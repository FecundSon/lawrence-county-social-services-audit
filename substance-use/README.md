3. **Freedom Healthcare Services' Ellwood City phone is 724-201-0850; 412-221-1091 is Freedom's Bridgeville site, not Ellwood City.** Own site (freedomtreatment.com) settles it. The Ellwood City address (773 Pershing St) was verified by exact geocoding as Lawrence County.
4. **The Lawrence County Drug and Alcohol Commission (Single County Authority) assesses, refers and contracts treatment; it does not directly provide inpatient detox or rehabilitation.** Own site (lawsca.org) lists what it provides internally vs what it contracts out — directory listings must not present contracted services as its own.
5. **Highland House is for men aged 18+ only, long-term residential / halfway house, referral from an inpatient programme required** (own site hhnc.org). Two phones conflict (724-654-7760 vs 724-936-4793) — neither confirmed.
6. **White Deer Run of New Castle has two conflicting addresses in circulation** — 413 Highland Avenue (older aggregators) vs 25 North Mill Street, Suite 304 (current SAMHSA-derived listings); phone 724-657-9916 is consistent. No own-site page opened; flagged, not resolved.
7. **No dedicated detox or inpatient substance-use facility in Lawrence County was found in the evidence** — the SCA contracts detox/rehab out-of-county; nearest regional referral evidenced is White Deer Run, Allenwood (Union County, out of scope).
