## Headline findings (only own-site-evidenced, material corrections)

1. **Catholic Charities' Lawrence County office is at 6 North Mill Street, New Castle — not 413 Highland Avenue, as aggregators still list.** Catholic Charities' own site (ccpgh.org/services/counties) gives the Lawrence County address as 6 North Mill Street, New Castle, PA 16101, phone 724-658-5526, hours Tuesday–Friday 9am–4pm (closed noon–1pm), services: Basic Needs Assistance, Counseling, Pregnancy and Family Program, Senior Services. The 413 Highland Avenue address in SAMHSA-derived aggregator listings is stale.
2. **"Discovery House CTC New Castle" is now New Castle Comprehensive Treatment Center, and its own-site call number is (855) 841-4415.** The centre's own site (ctcprograms.com) gives the name New Castle Comprehensive Treatment Center, 2710 W. State St., New Castle, outpatient MAT (methadone, buprenorphine, Suboxone) for adults 18+, individual and group therapy, 24/7 appointment scheduling, walk-in inquiries accepted, open each morning Monday–Saturday. Two other numbers circulate for the same clinic — 724-598-7999 (SAMHSA/NPI record) and 724-923-4597 (aggregators) — and must be resolved before any send; do not publish any one of them as the clinic's number on this evidence alone.
3. **Freedom Healthcare Services' Ellwood City phone is 724-201-0850; 412-221-1091 is Freedom's Bridgeville site, not Ellwood City.** Freedom's own site (freedomtreatment.com) lists 724-201-0850 against 773 Pershing Street, Ellwood City, PA 16117, and 412-221-1091 against 316 Station Street, Bridgeville. Aggregators that give 412-221-1091 as the Ellwood City clinic phone are wrong. The Ellwood City address was verified by exact geocoding as Lawrence County.
4. **The Lawrence County Drug and Alcohol Commission (the Single County Authority) assesses, refers and contracts treatment; it does not directly provide inpatient detox or rehabilitation.** Its own site (lawsca.org/services) says it provides internally: Administration, Case Management (Assessment, Treatment Referral, Case Coordination), Prevention/Education, Student Assistance, Kauffman's Korner, Recovery Support Services and Intervention — and contracts with local and/or out-of-county providers for inpatient hospital and non-hospital detoxification and rehabilitation, partial hospitalization, halfway house, outpatient, intensive outpatient and medication-assisted treatment. Any directory listing that reads as if the SCA itself operates detox/rehab needs qualifying, not deleting.
5. **Highland House is for men aged 18+ only, is a long-term residential / halfway-house programme, and requires completion of and referral from an inpatient drug/alcohol programme.** Its own site (hhnc.org) says so in terms. Two different phones circulate for it — 724-654-7760 and 724-936-4793 — and the fetched own-site page showed no phone, so neither is confirmed here.
6. **White Deer Run of New Castle has two conflicting addresses in circulation: 413 Highland Avenue (older aggregators) and 25 North Mill Street, Suite 304 (current SAMHSA-derived listings).** The phone, 724-657-9916, is consistent across sources. No White Deer Run own-site page for the New Castle site was opened in this pilot, so the address conflict is flagged, not resolved. Note that 413 Highland Avenue is also the stale address for Catholic Charities (#9) — the two stale listings appear to share an old co-location.
7. **No dedicated detox or inpatient substance-use facility in Lawrence County was found in the evidence.** The SCA's own site confirms detox and rehabilitation are contracted out-of-county. The nearest regional residential/detox referral evidenced in this pilot is White Deer Run, Allenwood (Union County — out of scope, #28). Do not present any Lawrence County listing as offering detox unless its own site says so.

## What this report does NOT claim

- **Not a complete county directory.** This is a partial pilot: PA 211's JavaScript search pages cannot be pulled completely by text fetch, so the pilot is anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites. Other Lawrence County substance-use services may exist and are not in this report.
- **No treatment advice, and no claim that any programme works.** This report records contact details, stated services and intake routes only. It does not recommend any provider, level of care or medication, and it does not assess quality or outcomes.
- **No bed or placement availability.** Availability changes constantly. Only the intake process (who to call, referral requirements) is recorded, never "beds available".
- **No organisation is called closed or defunct.** Rows marked UNVERIFIED mean the evidence found was insufficient or conflicting — a failed page load or a failed search is not evidence of closure.
- **No private treatment details are published.** No individual, client or patient information appears in this report.
- **No confidential location is published.** The Crisis Shelter of Lawrence County is named only as a host site for SCA recovery support in the county plan; its address is not in this report.
- **Individual AA/NA meetings are not listed as services.** Only meeting-list sources are recorded, with times/venues expressly unverified.
- **Hours are only stated where a source stated them.** Absence of hours in an entry means the source did not state them, not that the service has no hours. In particular, hours for New Horizons and New Dawn rest on aggregators only and are not confirmed.
- **Phone numbers are as published by the cited source on 2026-11-22.** Numbers conflict across sources for several rows (notably Highland House, New Castle CTC, White Deer Run's address, and the VA clinic phone); conflicting numbers are flagged in the entries, not silently resolved.
- **County boundaries are by street address, not ZIP or town name.** Every in-county address was verified by exact geocoding; Ellwood City straddles the Lawrence/Beaver line and its one listing (Freedom Healthcare, 773 Pershing St) was geocoded to Lawrence County. Out-of-scope verdicts rest on the street address's county (Beaver Falls = Beaver; Sharon/Hermitage/Grove City = Mercer; Allenwood = Union; Youngstown = Ohio).
- **No in-county detox/inpatient facility is claimed.** None was found in the evidence; the SCA contracts detox and rehabilitation out-of-county. This is a gap in the pilot's coverage, not proof that no such facility exists.
