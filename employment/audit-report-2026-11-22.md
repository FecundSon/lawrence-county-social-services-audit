# Lawrence County, PA — Employment / Job Help Audit Report
Date checked: 2026-11-22
County: Lawrence County, PA (county seat New Castle, also the largest
city). Category: employment / job help.
Method: pilot list built from public directories (statewide PA 211
retrievable entries, West Central Job Partnership own site, pa.gov
OVR office directory fetched 2026-11-22, Lawrence County Community
Action Partnership, LCCTC, BC3, Lawrence County Learning Center,
Lawrence-Mercer Manufacturers Coalition, Lawrence County District
Attorney's Office, and county/chamber lists); every item then checked
against the organisation's own website/page, via public web search and
page-text fetches. Own site beats directory; directory beats a
third-party aggregator. County for every address was verified by exact
geocoding, never by ZIP or town name alone.
Status: NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211
or to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any
outward report. This audit does not refer to Keith as a local
resident of Lawrence County.
Recipient note: the directory is statewide PA 211 (pa211.org /
search.pa211.org) — NOT PA 211 East and NOT the Lancaster database
manager. Lawrence County sits in the West Central Workforce
Development Area (Lawrence and Mercer Counties), which is why
regional listings routinely mix the two counties — and why Sharon
(Mercer County) addresses appear in Lawrence County lists.

## Honest limit
PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull
a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on
retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own
sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211
Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.

1. **OVR New Castle has moved — PA 211's Frew Mill Road address is
   stale.** PA 211 lists New Castle Vocational Rehabilitation
   Services at 1745 Frew Mill Rd, Suite 1, New Castle, PA 16101,
   (724) 656-3070. The current pa.gov OVR office directory (fetched
   2026-11-22) lists the New Castle district office at 2656 Ellwood
   Rd, Suite #102, New Castle, PA 16101, with the same phones:
   724-656-3070 voice, 724-656-3252 TTY, toll-free 1-800-442-6379,
   video phone 724-510-0522, serving Armstrong, Beaver, Butler and
   Lawrence. Geocoding confirms both addresses are in Lawrence
   County — the move is within New Castle, not out of the county.
   Older OVR documents (2012–2021) list yet another address,
   100 Margaret St, New Castle. The Frew Mill Road address should
   not be published as current; confirm the Ellwood Road address in
   the live re-check before sending.
2. **BC3 @ Lawrence Crossing is closed — do not list 2849 W State St,
   New Castle, as a BC3 location.** BC3's own site (bc3.edu) states
   BC3 @ Lawrence "supersedes BC3 @ Lawrence Crossing in Union
   Township, which served as BC3's additional location in Lawrence
   County from 2008 until June 2025." The current location is
   BC3 @ Lawrence, 2658 Ellwood Rd, Suite 129, Shenango Commons,
   New Castle, PA 16101, (724) 658-1938, opened August 25, 2025
   (geocoded: Lawrence County, Shenango Township). Any listing that
   still gives the Lawrence Crossing address is stale.
3. **The Lawrence County Learning Center has moved — do not list
   101 S Mercer St, New Castle, as its address.** The centre's own
   site and BC3's own reporting agree it moved from 101 S Mercer
   St (the CareerLink building) to a space it sublets at BC3 @
   Lawrence, Suite 129, 2658 Ellwood Rd, Shenango Township, in
   November 2025. Contact: Rachel Klausen, 724-906-4811. It is a
   connector, not a direct class provider: it does not offer classes
   directly, runs ed2go online courses, and lends laptops on site
   at BC3 for job applications and virtual interviews.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. A named
  Lawrence County EARN contractor, a separate Experience Works SCSEP
  listing, and the Community Connections youth re-entry programme are
  UNVERIFIED from own sites in these searches — that is a gap in
  public web evidence, not a finding.
- It does not claim the old CareerLink/WCJP addresses (44 S Beaver
  St; 102 Margaret St), the old OVR address (1745 Frew Mill Rd, and
  before that 100 Margaret St), BC3 @ Lawrence Crossing
  (2849 W State St) or the Learning Center's old 101 S Mercer St
  address are "closed" as organisations — only that they must not be
  published as current locations. The live re-check should confirm
  each before any send.
- It does not claim a programme exists in Lawrence County because a
  directory lists it for Lawrence. Most employment help for Lawrence
  residents is delivered from New Castle itself, but OVR blindness/
  visual services come from Erie, WIPA from Camp Hill, Goodwill
  career services from Pittsburgh, and SCSEP through a regional
  operator with no Lawrence office; the report says so for each item
  rather than implying a Lawrence walk-in office.
- It does not publish a funder/operator as a second centre (WCJP),
  a class site as a headquarters (Lifelong Learning Choices classes;
  BC3 @ Lawrence), a regional HQ as a local office (Goodwill SWPA
  Pittsburgh; AHEDD Camp Hill; PathStone Bradford), a job board as a
  service (Chamber), a private tuition school as free public help
  (New Castle School of Trades), or a narrow programme as general
  help (LCCAP employment support is case-managed; CAA Work Ready /
  EARN are TANF/referral-based; SCSEP is 55+; AMP is grades 11–12;
  the DA reentry programme is for convicted felons living in
  Lawrence County; Lark is for people with disabilities).
- It does not settle county from ZIP code or town name. New Castle,
  Sharon, Butler, Erie, Pittsburgh and Camp Hill addresses were each
  checked by exact geocoding (see pilot-list.md). The AAA ZIP error
  (16015 vs 16105) is itself proof that ZIP codes cannot be trusted
  to settle county or address.
- It does not give employment or benefits advice. It audits contact
  information only: names, addresses, phones, hours, and intake
  routes as organisations themselves state them.
- It gives no street address for any domestic-violence safe house.
  No DV service is in this category, and none was needed.
- It does not republish stale outcome claims as current: the DA
  reentry programme's "500+ employed" figure is dated August 1,
  2022; LMMC Manufacturing Readiness cohort dates shifted between
  the January and February 2026 newsletters; the SCSEP operator
  citation is the WCJP 2021–2024 Local Area Plan. All three are
  flagged for the live re-check.
- No page-text fetch in this run hit a rate limit; no finding
  depends on a retried fetch (see Evidence note above). The OVR
  relocation, BC3 Lawrence Crossing closure and Learning Center
  move rest on own-site pages as returned in search results plus
  the successfully fetched pa.gov OVR directory, and are flagged
  for live confirmation before any send.
