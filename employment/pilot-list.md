# Lawrence County, PA — Employment / Job Help Audit Pilot
Date started: 2026-11-22 — AUDIT COMPLETE, awaiting Keith's accept checkpoint.
Scope: job centres, career training, resume/interview help, vocational
rehabilitation, and supported employment serving Lawrence County, PA
(county seat New Castle, also the largest city; other towns Ellwood
City, New Wilmington, Wampum, Bessemer, New Beaver, Volant, Enon
Valley, Pulaski, Hillsville). This file is the candidate list from
public directories; findings are in audit-report-2026-11-22.md (same
folder). Nothing has been sent to PA 211 or any organisation. The
auditor is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County. Prepared with AI
assistance (Muse).

## Honest limit
PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull
a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on
retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own
sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211
Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.

## Rules applied to this list
- No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- Own site beats directory. Directory beats a third-party aggregator.
- No claim that an organisation is closed/defunct is made from a failed
  page load; such items are UNVERIFIED.
- A funder/operator name is not a second walk-in centre; a programme
  listed under a funder's name rather than the provider's name is a
  duplicate risk, flagged, not double-counted.
- Commercial staffing/recruitment firms are NOT counted as public
  employment help (see Excluded section).
- County is settled by exact-address geocoding, never by ZIP or town
  name alone. Sharon, Butler, Erie, Pittsburgh, Camp Hill and
  Shenango Township / Union Township addresses were each checked by
  exact geocoding. New Castle, PA is Lawrence County — do not confuse
  it with other New Castles elsewhere.
- No safe-house or confidential location is in this file.
- This audit does not refer to Keith as a local resident of Lawrence County.

## Excluded from this employment audit (not public employment help)
- Commercial staffing/recruitment firms with New Castle offices
  (e.g. Career Advantage Inc. of Lawrence County, 3009 Wilmington
  Rd, Ste D, New Castle; Spherion) — employers/recruiters, not free
  public employment-help services. Not counted, not presented as
  PA 211-style help.
- Individual employers' hiring pages and job boards (Indeed,
  LinkedIn, Career.io postings) — not services.
- Westminster College Career Center (New Wilmington, Lawrence
  County) — career services for enrolled students only, not public
  job help. Not counted.
- New Castle School of Trades satellite location (Pulaski, PA) and
  its East Liverpool, OH campus — outside Lawrence County's New
  Castle site; the New Castle campus (#16) is the audited item.
- The Lawrence County behavioural-health resource list
  (sites.google.com/view/bhnewcastle) is a candidate source, not a
  current directory; its entries were re-checked elsewhere (it is
  the source of the stale 44 S Beaver St WCJP address, #3, and a
  mistyped OVR phone, 724-646-3070, noted in the audit report).
