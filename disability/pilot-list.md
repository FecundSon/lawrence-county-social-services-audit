# Lawrence County, PA — Disability Services Pilot List
Date built: 2026-11-22
County: Lawrence County, PA (western Pennsylvania; county seat and largest city New Castle; other towns Ellwood City, Grove City (partly), Wampum, Bessemer, New Wilmington, Volant, Pulaski). Neighbouring counties: Mercer (N), Butler (E), Beaver (S) in PA; Columbiana and Mahoning counties in Ohio (W). Do NOT confuse New Castle, PA with other New Castles elsewhere.
Directory: statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
Status: PILOT LIST ONLY — as found BEFORE checking. Nothing here has been verified yet; see the audit report for verdicts. NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this audit has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County. Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

## Honest limit

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.

## County rule applied while building the list

Every address was checked by exact geocoding, never by ZIP or town name alone. Geocoding done 2026-11-22: 439 Countyline Street, New Castle = Lawrence County; 101 South Mercer Street, New Castle = Lawrence County; 1929 East Washington Street, New Castle = Lawrence County; 2656 Ellwood Road, New Castle = Lawrence County; 1745 Frew Mill Road, New Castle = Lawrence County; 1750 New Butler Road, New Castle = Lawrence County; 2665 Ellwood Road, New Castle = Lawrence County; 2706 Mercer Road, New Castle = Lawrence County (ZIP 16105); 2304 Wilmington Road, New Castle = Lawrence County; 453 Maple Street, Grove City = Mercer County; 4200 Lovell Place, Erie = Erie County; 1400 South Braddock Avenue, Pittsburgh = Allegheny County. Grove City, Erie, and Pittsburgh offices are kept only where they are the serving office for Lawrence residents, and are labelled as such. ZIP code does NOT settle county: PA 211 gives the Lawrence County AAA as "New Castle, PA 16015" while the organisation's own site gives 16105 — see the audit report.
