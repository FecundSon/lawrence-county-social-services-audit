**County:** Lawrence County, PA (county seat New Castle, largest city; other towns Ellwood City (part), New Wilmington, Wampum, Bessemer, Volant, Enon Valley, New Beaver, Pulaski)
**Category:** Mental health / crisis services
**Date checked:** 2026-11-22
**Directory:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org)
**Status:** NOT SENT YET — nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation. No organisation was contacted.
**Auditor:** not a local resident of Lawrence County. Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

> **HONEST LIMIT:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.

## Method

Public web only — search and page-text fetch; nobody contacted. Pilot list (28 rows) built from retrievable PA 211 record pages, the PA Department of Human Services county crisis-numbers list and county MH/ID offices list, the University of Kentucky East Palestine Health Research Program *Mental Health Resources Master Directory* (Lawrence County section), the Lawrence County Human Services Block Grant Plan, and organisations' own sites. Each listing was then checked against the organisation's own website or own page. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Every street address was verified by exact geocoding (county returned by the geocoder is recorded per row); towns in other counties — Hermitage (Mercer County), Butler (Butler County), Beaver Falls (Beaver County) — were treated as out of scope as *locations*, even where the service serves Lawrence County residents.

**No rate-limit interruption on 2026-11-22:** unlike the Snyder County check, page fetches did not hit a 429 limit in this check; the UNVERIFIED verdicts below are because an organisation's own site could not be opened or no own site was found, not because of a rate limit. That is stated per row.

**Verdicts:** CONFIRMED (directory claim matches own site) / CORRECTED (own-site evidence contradicts the directory claim on a material point) / CLARIFIED (claim is broadly right but misleading, incomplete, or needs a location/scope qualifier) / UNVERIFIED (no own-site evidence obtained) / OUT OF SCOPE (address outside Lawrence County with no Lawrence County site).

Note: entry numbers in this report follow the pilot list's numbering, so entries are matched by number and organisation name.

## Headline findings (only own-site-evidenced, material corrections)

1. **Human Services Center — Outpatient Services (PA 211 record) — wrong address and an impossible drop-in time.** PA 211 lists HSC Outpatient Services at **138 North Columbus Interbelt, New Castle, PA 16101**, with office hours Monday–Friday 8:00am–5:00pm and "Drop-In Intakes: Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 9:30pm". HSC's own site gives its address as **130 West North Street, New Castle, PA 16101** on its Get Help and crisis pages, and no HSC page checked gives 138 North Columbus Interbelt as an outpatient address. A 9:30pm drop-in intake is also irreconcilable with 8:00am–5:00pm office hours. **Correction: outpatient address 130 West North Street (not 138 North Columbus Interbelt, unless HSC confirms a second site); drop-in intakes almost certainly 8:30am–9:30am, not 9:30pm — confirm both on the live re-check before any send.**

### County crisis line — flagged prominently

**The Lawrence County crisis line is 724-652-9000, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week**, held by the **Human Services Center (HSC)**. It is confirmed on HSC's own crisis page (humanservicescenter.net/services/crisis-intervention-services/, opened 2026-11-22: "The Crisis telephone number is 724-652-9000"), on HSC's Get Help page (opened 2026-11-22: "Lawrence County's ONLY 24/7 Mental Health Crisis Service … (724) 652-9000"), and on the PA DHS county crisis-numbers list ("Lawrence: 724-652-9000 Lawrence County Crisis Intervention"). It is a **separate number from 988** (the national Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, call or text 988) — the two must not be substituted for each other. Do **not** use (724) 658-3578 as a crisis number: that is HSC's intake/main number. No error in the crisis number itself was found in any source checked.

### Other material clarifications (not corrections)

- **HSC's own site contradicts itself on crisis walk-in hours.** The Get Help page says "Just walk in and get help right away! We have friendly staff on site 24/7" at 130 West North St; the Crisis Intervention page says walk-in services are "Monday through Friday, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m." Both cannot be the walk-in rule as stated. A May 2026 newspaper article (shared on Facebook) describes a "24/7 walk-in center" at HSC. This needs settling with HSC's own site / the live PA 211 record before any send describes walk-in hours — see rows 6–7.
- **UPMC Jameson's inpatient behavioural-health unit is not yet open, and its opening date has slipped in UPMC's own announcements.** UPMC's October 2024 release said "scheduled to open in late 2025"; its June 2025 release said "scheduled to open in 2026"; a November 2026 trade report says it is "expected to open in mid- to late 2027". Do not list it as an operating inpatient unit — see row 13.
- **Two HSC numbers circulate and must not be swapped.** (724) 658-3578 is the intake number on HSC's own site and in directories; (724) 510-3271 circulates as HSC's main phone (Cope Compass / Teryli directory, sourced from the provider). The crisis number is neither — it is 724-652-9000.
- **The county MH/DS office has two near-identical numbers in state sources.** PA DHS's county MH/ID offices page (opened 2026-11-22) gives 439 Countyline Street, **724-658-2538**; the DHS SSD contacts page gives the same address with administrator Joe Venasco on **724-658-2539**. The 2538 number is the published main number; 2539 appears to be the administrator's line. Do not "correct" one to the other without evidence — see row 10.
- **PSYCH-Med Associates is listed at two New Castle addresses under one phone number** (2616 Wilmington Rd and 26 Nesbitt Rd Ste 253, both (724) 652-2323). Which is current could not be settled from the sources checked — see row 21.
- **New Day Counseling has two different phone numbers in its own records.** Directories and BBB give (724) 923-6124; its NPI record gives location phone (724) 730-3505 and mailing phone (724) 923-6124. Do not send either as settled — see row 18.
- **Contact E.A.R.S. is a real Lawrence County helpline, but its currency is unverified and one secondary source misprints its area code as 412.** The 724 numbers are corroborated by the practice's own site (New Wilmington Psychotherapy) and suicide.org, but no current E.A.R.S. own site was found — see row 24.

## What this report does NOT claim

- It does **not** claim to be a complete directory of mental-health services in Lawrence County. PA 211's search pages could not be pulled in full by text fetch, so this is a partial pilot (see Honest Limit above).
- It does **not** claim any organisation is closed or defunct. Five listings are UNVERIFIED because their own sites could not be opened or no own site was found on 2026-11-22; a failed page load or a missing website is not evidence of closure.
- It does **not** give crisis or treatment advice, assess quality of care, or recommend any provider. It audits contact information only (numbers, addresses, hours, who is served).
- It does **not** claim the one CORRECTED item is settled for sending: the HSC outpatient address and the 9:30pm drop-in time both need a live-browser re-check against the current PA 211 record and HSC's own site.
- It does **not** claim waitlist, capacity, insurance-acceptance or seasonal status for any listing — none was evidenced on 2026-11-22, except where a provider's own material states it (e.g. Ascend's "currently accepting new patients" posts).
- It does **not** publish any clinician's personal phone number. Private practices are listed only where they are publicly listed as practices, with practice numbers only.
- It does **not** claim language access beyond what is stated: Carelon states translators are available for Lawrence County members (1-877-688-5975). No other listing in this pilot states language access on its own site in the sources checked; absence from this report is not evidence that a provider lacks it.
- It does **not** treat a service that *serves* Lawrence County residents as a Lawrence County *location*. The Angelus Hermitage office (Mercer County) and the Family Psychological Associates Butler office (Butler County) are flagged as out-of-county locations wherever they appear.
- It does **not** claim the UPMC Jameson inpatient unit is operating — it is under construction with an opening now expected mid-to-late 2027.
- Geocoding verified street addresses against county boundaries; it did not verify that a provider is currently operating at that address.
