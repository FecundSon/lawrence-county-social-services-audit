# Lawrence County, PA — Mental Health / Crisis Services: Pilot List

**County:** Lawrence County, PA (county seat New Castle, largest city; other towns Ellwood City (part), New Wilmington, Wampum, Bessemer, Volant, Enon Valley, New Beaver, Pulaski, SNPJ)
**Category:** Mental health / crisis services
**Date checked:** 2026-11-22
**Directory:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org)
**Status:** NOT SENT YET — nothing in this list has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation. No organisation was contacted.
**Auditor:** not a local resident of Lawrence County. Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

> **HONEST LIMIT:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.

## Method

Public web only — search and page-text fetch; nobody contacted. Pilot list (28 rows) built from retrievable PA 211 record pages, the PA Department of Human Services county crisis-numbers list and county MH/ID offices list, the University of Kentucky East Palestine Health Research Program *Mental Health Resources Master Directory* (Lawrence County section), the Lawrence County Human Services Block Grant Plan, and organisations' own sites. Each listing was then checked against the organisation's own website or own page. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Every street address was verified by exact geocoding (county returned by the geocoder is recorded per row); towns in other counties — Hermitage and Farrell (Mercer County), Butler (Butler County), Beaver Falls (Beaver County), Pittsburgh (Allegheny County) — were treated as out of scope as *locations*, even where the service serves Lawrence County residents. ZIP code alone was not treated as settling county.

## Notes on the pilot as found

- The pilot is deliberately HSC-heavy: the Human Services Center is Lawrence County's community mental-health centre and its crisis provider, and PA 211 carries it under several separate records (crisis, outpatient, school-based partial hospitalisation) at two different street addresses. Those records are kept as separate rows so the address question can be settled row by row.
- Two phone numbers circulate for HSC itself — (724) 658-3578 (intake) and (724) 510-3271 (main) — and two for the county MH/DS office — 724-658-2538 (main, PA DHS) and 724-658-2539 (administrator line, DHS SSD). Both pairs are one digit apart in the last digit in the county-office case; they are flagged in the audit report, not silently resolved.
- "Foundations Behavioral Health Services" (row 26) appears in the UKY Lawrence County directory by name only, with no Lawrence County address or phone in any source checked; the name belongs to organisations in Ohio (Mercer County, OH) and in Doylestown, Bucks County, PA. It is carried as a row so the name collision is on the record.
- No NAMI affiliate with a Lawrence County meeting was found in the sources checked; no separate NAMI row is included. If a live-browser check finds one, it should be added.
