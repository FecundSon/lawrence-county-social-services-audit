## Headline corrections

1. **Human Services Center — Outpatient Services (PA 211 record) — wrong address and an impossible drop-in time.** PA 211 lists HSC Outpatient Services at **138 North Columbus Interbelt, New Castle**, with "Drop-In Intakes: Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 9:30pm". HSC's own site gives its address as **130 West North Street, New Castle**, and no HSC page checked gives 138 North Columbus Interbelt as an outpatient address. A 9:30pm drop-in intake is irreconcilable with 8:00am–5:00pm office hours. Correction: outpatient address 130 West North Street (unless HSC confirms a second site); drop-in intakes almost certainly 8:30am–9:30am, not 9:30pm. Both points need a live re-check before any send.

## County crisis line

**Lawrence County crisis line: 724-652-9000, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week**, held by the Human Services Center (HSC). Confirmed on HSC's own crisis page and Get Help page (opened 2026-11-22) and on the PA DHS county crisis-numbers list. It is separate from 988 (national Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) — the two must not be substituted for each other. Do not use (724) 658-3578 as a crisis number; that is HSC's intake/main number.

## Key clarifications

- **HSC's own site contradicts itself on crisis walk-in hours** — the Get Help page says staff are on site 24/7 for walk-ins at 130 West North St; the Crisis Intervention page says walk-in services are Monday–Friday 9:00am–5:00pm. Neither version should be sent as settled until the live re-check.
- **UPMC Jameson's inpatient behavioural-health unit is not yet open, and its opening date has slipped twice** in UPMC's own announcements: late 2025, then 2026, now mid-to-late 2027 (trade report, Nov 2026). Do not list it as an operating inpatient unit.
- **Two HSC numbers circulate and must not be swapped:** (724) 658-3578 (intake) and (724) 510-3271 (main phone in provider-sourced directories). The crisis number is neither — it is 724-652-9000.
- **The county MH/DS office has two near-identical numbers in state sources:** 724-658-2538 (main, current PA DHS page, 439 Countyline St) vs 724-658-2539 (administrator line, DHS SSD page). Do not "correct" one to the other.
- **PSYCH-Med Associates is listed at two New Castle addresses under one phone number** (2616 Wilmington Rd and 26 Nesbitt Rd Ste 253, both (724) 652-2323); which is current could not be settled.
- **New Day Counseling has two different phone numbers in its own records** ((724) 923-6124 in directories/BBB vs (724) 730-3505 as location phone in its NPI record).
- **Contact E.A.R.S. helpline (724) 658-5529 / teenline (724) 657-8255 is corroborated but its currency is unverified**, and one secondary source misprints the area code as 412 — never use 412-658-5529.
- **"Foundations Behavioral Health Services" is a name collision, not a Lawrence County provider** (the organisations of that name are in Ohio and in Doylestown, Bucks County).
- **Angelus's Hermitage office is in Mercer County**, not Lawrence County; the Family Psychological Associates Butler office is in Butler County.
- **The CARES Center / Next Step is a peer drop-in and advocacy centre, not a clinical provider** — and a secondary source gives its closing time as 3:45pm vs 4:00pm on its own site.

## Method and limits

Public web only — search and page-text fetch; nobody contacted. Pilot of 28 listings built from retrievable PA 211 records, the PA DHS county crisis-numbers list and county MH/ID offices list, the University of Kentucky East Palestine Health Research Program *Mental Health Resources Master Directory* (Lawrence County section), the Lawrence County Human Services Block Grant Plan, and organisations' own sites. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Every street address was verified by exact geocoding against county boundaries.

**Limit encountered on 2026-11-22:** no rate-limit interruption (unlike the Snyder County check); the five UNVERIFIED listings are unverified because their own sites could not be opened or no own site was found — Lawrence County Mental Health & Wellness, New Day Counseling, Blue Archer Therapy, Ascend Mental Health of PA (no own website found, only the practice's own social posts), and Family Psychological Associates (no Lawrence County office found on the provider's own material). They are marked UNVERIFIED — not confirmed, and not called closed or defunct. Their addresses geocode to Lawrence County; phones/hours rest on directory/social sources only.

No crisis or treatment advice is given anywhere in this audit; contact information only. No clinician's personal phone is published. Waitlist, capacity, insurance and seasonal status were not evidenced for any listing.
