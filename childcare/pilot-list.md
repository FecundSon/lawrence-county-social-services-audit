## How this list was built

PA 211 taxonomy searches retrievable by text fetch returned Lawrence County Community Action Partnership (LCCAP) Early Learning items — The Learning Tree Childcare and Preschools, Head Start and Early Head Start site lists, and the Lawrence County YMCA Child Development Center and Summer Day Camp. No complete PA 211 county-filtered child-care list could be pulled. The pilot was therefore built from: (a) retrievable PA 211 items, (b) LCCAP's own locations / early-learning pages (lccap.org), (c) pa.gov ELRC county list and county MH/ID offices list, (d) Lawrence County government pages (Children and Youth Services, Domestic Relations, MH/DS), and (e) secondary child-care listings (childcarecenter.us, Daycare.com, Care.com, TrustedCare, Meta place listings, Yellow Pages, school listings). Claim sources are listed per row. Nothing below has yet been verified against an own site at this stage — verification is in `audit-report-2026-11-22.md`.

Neighbouring counties for scope checks: Mercer (N), Butler (E), Beaver (S) in PA; Columbiana and Mahoning in Ohio (W). Ellwood City straddles Lawrence and Beaver Counties — every Ellwood City address in this pilot was geocoded by exact street address and confirmed in Lawrence County (see audit report). New Castle, PA is not to be confused with other New Castles elsewhere. ZIP code alone was not used to settle county.

## Notes on the pilot as found

- **No waitlist status was stated on any own site checked** for this pilot, except YMCA "space is limited" posts and Rosie's / Parents' Preschool enrolment posts, which are dated promotional claims, not a current waitlist fact. Do not publish waitlist claims.
- **No child-care centre was found in the retrievable sources for** Volant, New Bedford, Wampum, Enon Valley, West Pittsburg, Hillsville, Harlansburg, New Galilee, Bessemer (other than Mohawk Elementary Head Start), or Edinburg — absence from these sources, not proof none exists.
- **School-year vs summer:** LCCAP Head Start / Pre-K Counts sites are school-year programmes; YMCA Y-Zone and Bright Star / Pre-K Kids summer care were not verified per site for summer hours.
- **Conflicting secondary claims carried into the pilot (to be resolved in the audit report):** Bright Star main-centre address (1929 vs 1315 Suite A vs 646 E Washington St) and hours (6:00am–5:30pm vs 6:00am–6:00pm); Bright Star Two phone ((724) 965-8048 vs (724) 657-9221); Tender Care phone ((724) 656-9350 vs (724) 670-3055) and hours (6:00am–6:30pm vs 6:00am–5:30pm); Frew Mill Early Head Start phone ((724) 202-6775 vs (724) 658-7258 ext 5706); Neshannock Head Start phone ((724) 856-4674 vs LCCAP main (724) 658-7671 vs school main (724) 652-8709); Mohawk Elementary town/ZIP (Bessemer, PA 16112 vs New Castle, PA 16102); Blessings From Above phone ((724) 856-3798 vs (724) 202-7651 vs (724) 652-2240); Domestic Relations phone ((724) 656-2179 vs (724) 658-5651); MH/DS phone ((724) 658-2538 vs (724) 658-2539); Parents' Preschool hours (aggregator 9:00am–3:00pm vs own-site session times).
- **A conflicting ELRC claim was found and excluded as a separate row:** several "best daycares" aggregator pages say ELRC Region 5 serves Lawrence County. pa.gov and elrc3.com both place Lawrence in **ELRC Region 3** (Region 5 is Allegheny County only). The pa.gov/elrc3.com position is used; the Region 5 claim is flagged in the audit report, not treated as a second ELRC for Lawrence.
- **A stale operator name is carried in directories:** headstartprograms.org still lists the Head Start grantee as "Lawrence County Social Services, Inc."; the organisation is now Lawrence County Community Action Partnership (LCCAP), lccap.org. Flagged in the audit report.
- **Head Start site names found in aggregators but NOT on LCCAP's own locations page** (Croton / 420 Fern St, East Lawrence Elementary, Northside Elementary, Hartman Elementary, and the older "Community Develop Institute Head Start, 301 E Long Ave") were not given separate pilot rows; they are flagged in the audit report's pre-send list for the live PA 211 check.
- No children's personal information or photos were collected or published.
