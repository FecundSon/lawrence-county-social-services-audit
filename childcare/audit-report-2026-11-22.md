## What this report does NOT claim

- It does **not** claim to be a complete directory of child care / family support in Lawrence County. PA 211's JavaScript search pages could not be pulled completely by text fetch; this is a partial pilot.
- It does **not** claim any organisation is closed or defunct. ABC Learning Center and Cuddly Bears Day Care are UNVERIFIED, not closed — ABC's expired-licence snapshot and Cuddly Bears' single-aggregator "temporarily closed" flag are not own-site evidence.
- It does **not** claim waitlist status, fees, or current enrolment openings for any provider — none was stated on an own site checked. YMCA "space is limited", Rosie's "NOW ENROLLING" and Parents' Preschool "openings" are dated promotional claims, not current availability facts.
- It does **not** claim hours for programmes whose own site gave only facility hours (YMCA Downtown: 5:30am–9:00pm is facility hours, not CDC hours) or only a school-day schedule (LCCAP Head Start / Pre-K Counts sites are school-year programmes; summer hours were not verified per site).
- It does **not** publish any child's personal information or photos; none was collected.
- Addresses were verified by exact street address (geocoded) against own sites / school listings, not by ZIP or town name alone. Ellwood City addresses were each geocoded to Lawrence County (the borough straddles Lawrence and Beaver Counties). Hermitage (Mercer County) and Northampton (Lehigh County) listings were excluded as out of county (rows 29–30).
- "CONFIRMED" means the address/phone/service matched an own site (or, for row 27, a pa.gov record with independent corroboration) on 2026-11-22. It is a snapshot, not a guarantee the details have not changed since.
- The Lawrence County YMCA childcare page (lawcoymca.org/programs/childcare) returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-22 and was not re-attempted; YMCA CDC findings rest on PA 211, ymca.org, and the YMCA's own 2021 programme guide instead.
