# Lawrence County, PA — Social Services Listing Audits

**All categories checked: 2026-11-22. Status: staged for publication in
Keith's own repo first. Nothing in this audit has been sent to PA 211
or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
One county, twelve public categories — a dated curation audit of
public social-services listings for Lawrence County, PA (a western
Pennsylvania county on the Ohio border; county seat and largest city
New Castle; other towns Ellwood City, New Wilmington, Wampum,
Bessemer, Volant, Pulaski, Enon Valley, New Bedford, West Pittsburg,
Edinburg, Harlansburg and New Galilee; neighbouring counties Mercer
(N), Butler (E) and Beaver (S) in PA, Columbiana and Mahoning in
Ohio (W)), checked against the organisations' own websites and other
public listings. It is not a replacement directory: it does not claim
to be a current list of open services, and it should not be used as
one without checking with the organisation or PA 211 first.

Each category folder holds three files: a README with the headline
corrections, the full audit report with evidence and sources, and the
original pilot list it was checked against.

The auditor is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County. This audit was
prepared with assistance from an AI assistant, Muse. Every report
documents its provenance and limits; nothing was invented, imputed, or
deleted silently, and no organisation is called closed or defunct from
a failed page load — anything not confirmed from the organisation's
own site is marked UNVERIFIED.

## Honest limit — read this first
PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch could
not pull a complete Lawrence County-filtered list, so these are partial
pilots anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists and
the organisations' own websites — NOT complete county directories.
**A live-browser check of search.pa211.org filtered to Lawrence County
is still outstanding before any send.** Each report carries a pre-send
re-check list. Several checks also rest on own-site text returned by
search rather than full page fetches, or on pages that returned 403;
those items are flagged in their reports and must be re-checked live
before any send.

## Cross-cutting findings
- **The County Assistance Office moved.** Per the county
  commissioners' minutes of September 22, 2026, the Lawrence County
  Assistance Office closed 108 Cascade Galleria after September 25
  and opened at **2656 Ellwood Road, Suite 103, New Castle**, on
  September 28, 2026 (phone 724-656-3000 unchanged). The same
  Ellwood Road address now also houses OVR New Castle (Suite 102)
  and BC3 @ Lawrence (Suite 129, 2658 Ellwood Rd). Every directory
  retrieved still lists Cascade Galleria.
- **The Area Agency on Aging ZIP is 16105, not 16015.** PA 211 lists
  2706 Mercer Road, Shenley Square, New Castle, PA 16015 in record
  after record (seniors, disability, employment, legal aid,
  transportation); the correct ZIP is **16105** (agency's own site,
  PA Dept of Aging lists, exact geocoding).
- **ERAP is stale and PAHAF is closed.** ERAP1 ended September 30,
  2022 and ERAP2 ended September 30, 2025; PAHAF takes no new
  applications. Both should be retired from live help listings.
- **LIHEAP's 2026–27 season is open** (Nov 2, 2026 – Apr 23, 2027);
  LIHWAP is closed (federal programme ended 2023–24).
- **ZIP code does not settle county.** Several "Lawrence County"
  listings geocode to Beaver, Mercer, Butler or Washington Counties
  (e.g. the Lillyville food pantry geocodes to Beaver County), and
  Ellwood City straddles the Lawrence/Beaver line, so every address
  was checked by exact geocoding, never by ZIP or town name alone.

## Headline corrections (one per category)
- **Food:** People in Need's pantry runs 2nd Wednesday 12:30–3:30pm,
  not 2–4pm (own site); Salvation Army New Castle is 240 W Grant St,
  not 204; LCCAP's own "Public Food Pantries" page lists only 6
  pantries, omitting three that are on LCCAP's own Facebook flyer;
  no walk-in pantry could be confirmed at the Salvation Army Ellwood
  City Service Center.
- **Shelters:** Hope Place Women's Shelter intake is **724-652-4349**,
  not the City Rescue Mission's main number; Arise is confirmed by
  hotline only (724-652-9036) and the Western PA CoC list gives the
  wrong Arise hotline (724-656-7867); "Crisis Shelter of Lawrence
  County" is Arise's former name, not a second shelter, and "Women's
  Shelter of Lawrence County" is a duplicate listing of the same
  organisation; no Family Promise affiliate and no standalone
  winter/Code Blue overnight shelter serve the county.
- **Mental health / crisis:** The county crisis line is **724-652-9000,
  24/7** (Human Services Center) — do not use (724) 658-3578 as a
  crisis number (that is HSC's intake line). HSC Outpatient Services
  is at 130 West North Street, not 138 North Columbus Interbelt, and
  its drop-in intakes are almost certainly 8:30–9:30am, not 9:30pm.
  UPMC Jameson's inpatient behavioural-health unit is not yet open
  (opening now mid-to-late 2027).
- **Housing:** PA 211 has LCCAP's phone and fax swapped on the Rent
  Assistance listing (phone (724) 658-7258, fax (724) 658-7664);
  Catholic Charities' Lawrence office is at 6 North Mill Street, not
  119 East North Street; the Housing Authority's Section 8 waitlist
  status is unresolved — publish neither open nor closed.
- **Utilities:** PPL does not serve Lawrence County — the electric
  utility is Penn Power (FirstEnergy); the gas utility is Columbia
  Gas, not Peoples. FirstEnergy WARM intake is 1-888-282-6816.
  PA 211's PA American Water H2O Help to Others figures are stale.
- **Employment:** OVR New Castle moved to 2656 Ellwood Rd, Suite 102;
  BC3 @ Lawrence Crossing closed June 2025 (now BC3 @ Lawrence,
  2658 Ellwood Rd, Suite 129); the Learning Center moved there too;
  do not list 44 S Beaver St or 102 Margaret St as CareerLink — the
  current office is 101 S Mercer St, Suite 101.
- **Child care:** Lawrence County is ELRC **Region 3** (aggregators
  saying Region 5 are wrong — Region 5 is Allegheny County only).
  Parents' Preschool of Ellwood City is a sessional preschool, not
  9:00am–3:00pm. Several phone/address conflicts (Bright Star,
  Tender Care, Blessings From Above) are unresolved, not settled.
- **Seniors:** Only two senior centres serve Lawrence County
  (Challenges: New Castle and Ellwood City). Meals on Wheels of New
  Castle moved to 3345 Wilmington Rd, (724) 674-2320. Farmers Market
  vouchers for 2026 end November 30, 2026 — do not publish as open
  without a 2027 check.
- **Disability:** Disability Options Network (DON), the county's Center
  for Independent Living, moved to 101 S Mercer St, Suite 104 (DON
  Services, Inc. is Suite 210 in the same building — do not confuse
  the suites). Lawrence's TechOWL ATRC is CLASS, Pittsburgh, not UCP
  Central PA, Camp Hill.
- **Substance use:** Catholic Charities' office is 6 North Mill Street,
  not 413 Highland Avenue. "Discovery House CTC New Castle" is now
  New Castle Comprehensive Treatment Center, own-site number
  (855) 841-4415. No dedicated detox or inpatient substance-use
  facility in Lawrence County was found — the Single County Authority
  contracts detox/rehab out of county.
- **Legal aid:** Neighborhood Legal Services' Lawrence office moved to
  101 South Mercer Street, Suite 105 (not 125 East North Street),
  intake Mon–Thu 9:00am–12:00pm only. Laurel Legal Services merged
  into Summit Legal Aid in 2023 and Summit's general legal aid does
  NOT serve Lawrence County — NLS is the provider. The PBA Lawyer
  Referral Service is a PAID referral ($30 max for a 30-minute
  consult), not free aid.
- **Transportation:** The MATP administrator is Lawrence County Social
  Services, Inc. (LCSS) under the LCCAP umbrella, booking
  (724) 658-7258. Senior passes ARE valid on the Pittsburgh commuter
  bus (NCATA Route 71, $4.00). PA 211 appears to swap voice and fax
  on the ACTS Senior Shared Ride record. Greyhound in New Castle is
  a Park & Ride bus stop, not a station, and Route 91 is no
  Youngstown link.

## What this audit does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. Items
  that could not be confirmed from an organisation's own site are
  marked UNVERIFIED, not closed.
- It does not claim to be a complete directory of Lawrence County
  services. See "Honest limit" above.
- Bed availability, waitlist positions, and seasonal status are
  date-stamped to 2026-11-22 and change constantly; check with the
  organisation before relying on them.
- Fares and schedules are date-stamped to 2026-11-22.
- It does not rewrite expert advisory content (medical, mental-health,
  substance-use, or legal advice). Only contact information,
  structure, and metadata were audited.
