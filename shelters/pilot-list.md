# Lawrence County, PA — Shelters Pilot List

**County:** Lawrence County, Pennsylvania (county seat New Castle, the
largest city; other towns: Ellwood City (partly in Beaver County),
New Wilmington, Wampum, Bessemer, Volant, New Beaver, Enon Valley,
Pulaski, West Pittsburg). Western Pennsylvania, on the Ohio border.
Neighbouring counties: Mercer (N), Butler (E), Beaver (S) in PA;
Columbiana and Mahoning counties in Ohio (W). **ZIP code does NOT
settle county — exact-address geocoding does.** Do NOT confuse
New Castle, PA with other New Castles elsewhere, and do NOT confuse
Lawrence County, PA with Family Promise of Lawrence, Kansas, which
surfaces in "Lawrence" shelter searches and is OUT OF SCOPE.

**Category:** Shelters (emergency, family, youth, veterans).
**Directory:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date compiled:** 2026-11-22. **Status: not sent yet. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to PA 211 or any organisation. No organisation was contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse.

## How this list was built

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-22
did not return a complete extractable Lawrence County-filtered result list.
This pilot is therefore a **partial pilot**, anchored on:

- PA 211 search results retrievable by text fetch (Overnight Shelters,
  Domestic Violence Shelters, Human Trafficking Shelters, Sexual Assault
  Shelters, Recovery Homes/Halfway Houses, Sober Living Homes, Family
  Permanent Supportive Housing, Eviction Prevention searches, and
  individual PA 211 records for the City Rescue Mission, Arise, the
  Lawrence County Community Action Partnership, and the Veterans
  Leadership Program);
- the Inglis / PHFA Lawrence County Quick Start Housing and Services
  Information guide;
- the Western Pennsylvania Continuum of Care Coordinated Entry
  Assessment Centers list (pennsylvaniacoc.org, "Copy of CE Assessment
  Centers 24.11.27" file, retrieved 2026-11-22);
- the PA Department of Human Services Homeless Assistance Program
  contact list (pa.gov);
- the VA FY2026 SSVF provider intake list (via wethepeoplesfoundation.org,
  reviewed 17 Oct 2026);
- shelterlistings.org Lawrence County page and the Mercer County Crisis
  Resource Directory (Oct 2023) (secondary — never treated as
  authoritative);
- organisations' own websites, as returned by search and by direct
  page-text fetch on 2026-11-22.

Each item below records the directory claim as found, and the question that
was open before checking. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org filtered
to Lawrence County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.

**Honest limit:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch
cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot
anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus
organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser
PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.

**Safety note:** One item in this pilot (Arise, item 9) is a
domestic-violence emergency shelter in a confidential location. It is listed
by 24-hour hotline and business phone only; no shelter address is published
anywhere in these files. PA 211 lists this record with a New Castle street
address; that address is NOT repeated in these files and must never be
treated as the shelter location — it is the organisation's business office
at most. Two further items (items 10 and 11) are the same organisation
under an old name and under a secondary-listing name; they carry no street
address in these files either.

## Gaps noted while building the list (not listings)

- **Lawrence County DOES have general emergency shelter buildings, unlike
  some rural counties audited before.** The City Rescue Mission runs a
  men's emergency shelter (Crossroads, 319 S Croton Ave) and a women's
  shelter (Hope Place, 1018 Rebecca St), both in New Castle, plus a men's
  recovery residence; Arise runs a domestic-violence emergency shelter
  reached only by hotline (confidential location). A Lawrence County
  resident needing an emergency shelter bed is not automatically referred
  out of county.
- **No dedicated family shelter beyond Hope Place (women with children)
  and Arise (survivors with children) was found.** No Family Promise
  affiliate serves Lawrence County, PA: every "Family Promise of
  Lawrence" result is Lawrence, Kansas (see out-of-scope item 29). This
  is a gap in provision, not a listing error.
- **No standalone winter / Code Blue overnight shelter was found for
  Lawrence County.** The only winter provision found is the City Rescue
  Mission's 24/7 shelter doors (its Nov 2025 cold-weather post says "OUR
  DOORS ARE OPEN 24 HOURS A DAY, 7 DAYS A WEEK", (724) 652-4321) and a
  one-off emergency warming centre at George Washington School during the
  January 2026 power outage (item 22). No recurring Code Blue programme
  with published dates was found. That absence is a gap in provision, not
  proof that no informal arrangement exists.
- **No separate youth homeless shelter for unaccompanied/runaway youth
  was found.** Krause Youth Center (item 13) is a placement residential
  programme for youth removed from home, not a walk-in shelter for
  homeless youth.
- **No Salvation Army shelter in Lawrence County.** The New Castle Corps
  and the Ellwood City Service Unit offer emergency assistance, not an
  on-site shelter confirmed on the Salvation Army's own site.
