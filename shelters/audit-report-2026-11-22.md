# Lawrence County, PA — Shelter Listing Audit

**County:** Lawrence County, Pennsylvania (county seat New Castle, the
largest city; other towns: Ellwood City (partly in Beaver County),
New Wilmington, Wampum, Bessemer, Volant, New Beaver, Enon Valley,
Pulaski, West Pittsburg). Western Pennsylvania, on the Ohio border.
Neighbouring counties: Mercer (N), Butler (E), Beaver (S) in PA;
Columbiana and Mahoning counties in Ohio (W).
**Category:** Shelters (emergency, family, youth, veterans).
**Directory checked against:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date checked:** 2026-11-22.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide
PA 211, to any shelter, or to any other directory. No organisation was
contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse.

## Method and limits — please read before using this

- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-22. No shelter, no
  directory, and no one else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party
  directories. Where an organisation's own site did not state a fact, that
  item is marked CLARIFIED or UNVERIFIED in the table — a gap in public web
  evidence, not a finding, and it must not be published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or
  missing page is reported as unverified, never as a closure. In particular,
  one place listing flags a Krause Youth Shelter entry at an older address
  as "Temporarily Closed" — that flag is NOT treated as a closure finding
  (see item 13).
- A live website does not by itself prove a shelter is currently operating.
- Office hours from a church/office/centre page are not shelter hours, and
  are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- **Bed availability is never published as a static fact in this audit.**
  Only the intake phone and intake process are reported.
- **One shelter in this audit is a domestic-violence shelter in a
  confidential location (item 9, Arise). Its shelter address is NOT
  published anywhere in these files.** It is counted (1 confidential-
  location item) and its public 24-hour hotline (724-652-9036) and public
  business phone (724-652-9206) are given, because the organisation itself
  publishes those. PA 211 lists this record with a New Castle street
  address; that address is NOT repeated in these files and must never be
  treated as the shelter location. It is the organisation's business
  office at most, and the shelter itself is in a confidential location.
  The shelter location must never be added to a public copy of this audit.
- **Limit, stated plainly:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven,
  and a text fetch on 2026-11-22 did not return a complete extractable
  Lawrence County-filtered result list. This is therefore a **partial
  pilot**, anchored on PA 211 items retrievable by text fetch plus the
  Inglis/PHFA Lawrence County guide, the Western Pennsylvania Continuum of
  Care Coordinated Entry Assessment Centers list, the PA DHS Homeless
  Assistance Program list, the VA FY2026 SSVF provider list, and
  organisations' own sites. It is NOT a complete county directory, and a
  live-browser check of search.pa211.org filtered to Lawrence County is
  still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- **Honest limit:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text
  fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial
  pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus
  organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser
  PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.
- Unlike some rural counties audited before, **Lawrence County does have
  general emergency shelter buildings of its own**: the City Rescue Mission
  runs a men's emergency shelter (Crossroads) and a women's shelter
  (Hope Place), both in New Castle, and Arise runs a domestic-violence
  emergency shelter reached only by hotline. A Lawrence County resident
  needing an emergency shelter bed is not automatically referred out of
  county.
- ZIP code does not settle county. Every in-county address below was
  checked against its exact address by geocoding, not its ZIP code.
  Geocoding confirmed: 319 S Croton Ave, New Castle — Lawrence County;
  1018 Rebecca St, New Castle — Lawrence County; 241 W Grant St,
  New Castle — Lawrence County; 430 Court St, New Castle — Lawrence
  County; 108 Cascade Galleria, New Castle — Lawrence County;
  481 Neshannock Ave, New Castle — Lawrence County; 240 W Grant St,
  New Castle — Lawrence County; 439 Countyline St, New Castle — Lawrence
  County; 708 Highland Ave, New Castle — Lawrence County; 332 Highland
  Ave, New Castle — Lawrence County; 511 Lawrence Ave, Ellwood City —
  Lawrence County (Ellwood City straddles Lawrence and Beaver Counties;
  this address is on the Lawrence side). Out-of-county checks: 111 S Cliff
  St, Butler — Butler County; 660 Fisher Hill St, Sharon — Mercer County.
  One geocoder quirk to re-check, not a finding: geocoding "319 South
  Croton Avenue" returned the point as "319 N Croton Ave". Every source
  (the Mission's own site, PA 211, directories) says South Croton Avenue;
  the county is Lawrence either way, and no correction is claimed on the
  strength of the geocoder's street-direction label alone.

## Headline findings

1. **Hope Place Women's Shelter has a separate intake number that PA 211
   does not give.** PA 211 lists Hope Place (1018 Rebecca St, New Castle)
   with the Mission's main number, (724) 652-4321. The Mission's own
   Women's Ministry material says: "Prior to in-take, all guests are
   required to call **724-652-4349** to schedule an interview. Upon
   in-take a drug and alcohol test will be administered." The same
   724-652-4349 number is given for Hope Place in place listings and for
   the Mission's Covenant House transitional programme in the Mercer
   County Crisis Resource Directory (Oct 2023). PA 211 should list
   **724-652-4349** as the Hope Place intake number, not the main
   (724) 652-4321.
2. **Arise — confirmed by hotline only, and its location must stay
   confidential.** The domestic-violence emergency shelter serving
   Lawrence County is confirmed on the organisation's own site and on
   WomensLaw.org (updated 15 Jul 2026): 24-hour hotline **724-652-9036**,
   business phone (724) 652-9206, services free, emergency shelter for
   adults and children, plus transitional housing and rapid re-housing,
   all by calling the hotline. PA 211 lists this record with a New Castle
   street address; that address is NOT repeated here and must not be
   treated as the shelter location — it is the business office at most.
   PA 211 should list this record by hotline only, or clearly as an
   office / intake point, never as a shelter address.
3. **The Western Pennsylvania CoC list gives the WRONG Arise hotline.**
   The CoC's Coordinated Entry Assessment Centers list (retrieved
   2026-11-22) gives Arise's DV 24/7 hotline as **724-656-7867**. Arise's
   own site, PA 211, WomensLaw.org (updated 15 Jul 2026), Adagio Health
   and the Lawrence County District Attorney's community-services page
   all give **724-652-9036**. The 724-656-7867 number appears only in
   older sources (a PAAR resource guide updated Apr 2022;
   domesticshelters.org). The CoC list should be corrected to
   724-652-9036. This is a correction to the CoC list, not to PA 211 —
   PA 211 already has the right number.
4. **"Crisis Shelter of Lawrence County" is not a second shelter — it is
   Arise's former name — and "Women's Shelter of Lawrence County" is not
   a shelter at all.** PA 211's own providing-organisation line for Arise
   reads "Arise / Crisis Shelter of Lawrence County", and the
   organisation's history (serving Lawrence County since 1981) is given
   under both names. shelterlistings.org separately lists a "Women's
   Shelter Of Lawrence County" in New Castle at the same address as
   Arise's office. Directories carrying all three names are counting one
   organisation up to three times. The old name should be merged into the
   Arise record and the "Women's Shelter" listing removed.
5. **The Salvation Army New Castle address is 240 W Grant St, not
   204 W Grant St.** The Salvation Army's own site gives **240 W Grant
   St, New Castle, PA 16101, (724) 652-7921**. An older local resource
   list gives "204 W Grant St" for the same Corps. Directories still
   showing 204 W Grant St should be corrected to 240. The New Castle
   Corps offers emergency assistance, not an on-site shelter confirmed on
   the Salvation Army's own site.
6. **Krause Youth Center's current address is 430 Court St, not
   332 Highland Ave — and it is a placement programme, not a walk-in
   youth shelter.** Cray Youth & Family Services' own Krause Youth Center
   page (fetched 2026-11-22) gives **430 Court Street, New Castle, PA
   16101, 724-656-9029**, Supervisor Sean Scott, and describes Krause as
   "the only residential option for youth, ages 10–18, in our community.
   As a shelter and residential program, Krause houses youth who have
   been removed from their home due to abuse, neglect or out of control
   behaviors." Older lists still give 332 Highland Ave. Krause is a
   residential placement for youth removed from home, with counselling
   and life-skills programming — not a walk-in shelter for homeless or
   runaway youth. Directories should use 430 Court St and should not
   present Krause as a drop-in homeless-youth shelter. The "Temporarily
   Closed" flag on one place listing for the old Highland Ave entry is
   NOT a closure finding: the programme's own page is live with the
   Court St address.
7. **For Lawrence County veterans, there are THREE SSVF providers — and
   the Veterans Leadership Program DOES serve Lawrence County.** VLP's
   own county list and the VA FY2026 SSVF provider list (grant 13-PA-129)
   both include Lawrence County; intake (844) 857-8387, office
   (412) 481-8200. The other two providers are LCCAP itself, under its
   legal name Lawrence County Social Services, Inc. (VA grant 14-PA-277,
   intake (724) 658-7258 / 1-888-252-5104, serving Lawrence, Armstrong,
   Beaver, Butler, Clarion, Jefferson and Mercer Counties), and Soldier
   On (VA grant 14-PA-280, intake (866) 406-8449, remote intake, no
   Lawrence County office). Directories should not present VLP as the
   only SSVF route, and should not confuse the LCCAP SSVF record with a
   separate organisation — Lawrence County Social Services, Inc. is a
   division of LCCAP.
8. **LCCAP takes walk-ins in the morning — PA 211 says "call for
   intake" only.** LCCAP's own Homeless Services page says: walk into the
   main corporate office, 241 West Grant Street, New Castle, between
   8:30am–11:00am, no appointment needed; or call (724) 658-7258 to
   schedule an afternoon appointment; or use the Client Portal, available
   24/7. LCCAP also does street outreach and outreach at shelters. PA 211
   should add the morning walk-in window to the Coordinated Entry record.
9. **No Family Promise affiliate and no standalone winter/Code Blue
   overnight shelter serve Lawrence County.** Every "Family Promise of
   Lawrence" result is Lawrence, Kansas — there is no Family Promise of
   Lawrence County, PA. No recurring Code Blue programme with published
   dates was found for Lawrence County; the winter cover found is the
   City Rescue Mission's 24/7 shelter doors (its Nov 2025 cold-weather
   post: "OUR DOORS ARE OPEN 24 HOURS A DAY, 7 DAYS A WEEK",
   724-652-4321) and a one-off emergency warming centre at George
   Washington School during the January 2026 power outage. These are
   gaps in provision, not listing errors, and they are recorded here so
   they are not mistaken for oversights.

## What this report does NOT claim

- It does **not** claim to be a complete directory of Lawrence County shelters.
  PA 211's Lawrence County-filtered list could not be pulled in full by text
  fetch, and a live-browser check is still outstanding. Items may exist in
  PA 211 that are not in this pilot.
- It does **not** claim any organisation is closed or defunct. Nothing in
  this audit is a closure finding — including the Krause Youth Shelter
  entry flagged "Temporarily Closed" at its old Highland Ave address, and
  the City Rescue Mission's Covenant House / Covenant Cottage programmes,
  which are UNVERIFIED, not closed.
- It does **not** publish bed availability, waitlist position, or current
  occupancy for any shelter. Only intake phones and intake processes are
  reported.
- It does **not** publish the location of the Arise shelter, or of any other
  confidential domestic-violence safe house. One item in this audit
  (item 9) is counted only / listed by hotline and business phone only.
  The street address PA 211 shows on the Arise records is not repeated in
  these files.
- It does **not** claim the City Rescue Mission runs a shelter bed at
  241 W Grant St (that is LCCAP's Coordinated Entry / intake office), and
  it does **not** claim LCCAP runs a shelter bed: its own site describes
  street outreach, shelter outreach, case management, rapid rehousing and
  rental help, not an on-site shelter.
- It does **not** claim the Men's Recovery Program's "True Life" name or
  any Crossroads length-of-stay limit from the Mission's own site — those
  details come from a secondary aggregator (shelterlistings.org) and must
  be re-checked with the Mission before they are sent anywhere.
- It does **not** claim either Covenant House / Covenant Cottage programme
  is current, and it does not claim a Family Promise programme serves
  Lawrence County, PA — the first is UNVERIFIED, the second does not exist
  under that name in Pennsylvania.
- It does **not** claim the 724-656-7867 number is disconnected. It claims
  only that every current authoritative source found gives Arise's hotline
  as 724-652-9036, and that the Western PA CoC list still shows
  724-656-7867. Re-check with Arise before sending.
- It does **not** claim a standalone winter / Code Blue overnight shelter
  exists for Lawrence County — none was found, and that absence is a gap
  in provision, not proof that no informal arrangement exists.
- It does **not** claim the Lawrence County Housing Authority waiting-list
  status: secondary sources conflict and waiting lists open and close
  frequently. Call (724) 656-5100 to check.
- It does **not** claim the "319 N Croton Ave" street-direction label
  returned by geocoding is a correction: every source says 319 South
  Croton Avenue, and the county is Lawrence either way.
- Direct page-text fetch was NOT rate-limited on 2026-11-22: the City
  Rescue Mission's own Men's and Women's Ministry pages and the Cray
  Youth & Family Services Krause Youth Center page were fetched in full.
  Own-site claims for Arise, LCCAP, the Salvation Army, VLP and the
  Housing Authority rest on those organisations' own pages as returned by
  search on 2026-11-22, not on full page fetches. A live-browser re-check
