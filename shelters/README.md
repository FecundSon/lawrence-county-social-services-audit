# Lawrence County, PA — Shelters Audit (README)

**County:** Lawrence County, Pennsylvania (county seat New Castle, the
largest city; other towns include Ellwood City, New Wilmington, Wampum,
Bessemer, Volant). Western Pennsylvania, on the Ohio border.
Neighbouring counties: Mercer (N), Butler (E), Beaver (S) in PA;
Columbiana and Mahoning counties in Ohio (W).
**Category:** Shelters (emergency, family, youth, veterans).
**Directory checked against:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date checked:** 2026-11-22.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide
PA 211, to any shelter, or to any other directory. No organisation was
contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse.

## Headline corrections

1. **Hope Place Women's Shelter has a separate intake number that PA 211
   does not give.** PA 211 lists Hope Place (1018 Rebecca St, New Castle)
   with the City Rescue Mission's main number, (724) 652-4321. The
   Mission's own Women's Ministry material says guests must call
   **724-652-4349** to schedule an intake interview (with a drug and
   alcohol test at intake). PA 211 should list 724-652-4349 as the Hope
   Place intake number.
2. **Arise (domestic-violence emergency shelter, serving Lawrence County)
   is confirmed by hotline only: 24-hour hotline 724-652-9036, business
   phone (724) 652-9206.** Its shelter is in a confidential location; no
   shelter address is published in these files, and PA 211 should list the
   record by hotline only (or clearly as an office/intake point), never as
   a shelter address.
3. **The Western Pennsylvania CoC list gives the WRONG Arise hotline:
   724-656-7867.** Arise's own site, PA 211, WomensLaw.org (updated
   15 Jul 2026), Adagio Health and the county District Attorney's page all
   give 724-652-9036; 724-656-7867 appears only in older sources (a PAAR
   guide updated Apr 2022; domesticshelters.org). The CoC list should be
   corrected to 724-652-9036. This is a correction to the CoC list, not to
   PA 211.
4. **"Crisis Shelter of Lawrence County" is not a second shelter — it is
   Arise's former name** (same hotline 724-652-9036, same business phone
   724-652-9206, same history since 1981), and **"Women's Shelter of
   Lawrence County" is not a shelter at all** — it is a duplicate
   secondary listing of Arise under another name. Directories carrying all
   three names count one organisation up to three times.
5. **The Salvation Army New Castle address is 240 W Grant St, not
   204 W Grant St.** The Salvation Army's own site gives 240 W Grant St,
   New Castle, (724) 652-7921; an older local resource list gives
   204 W Grant St. No on-site shelter is confirmed for the New Castle
   Corps — it is emergency assistance, not a shelter.
6. **Krause Youth Center's current address is 430 Court St, not
   332 Highland Ave — and it is a placement programme, not a walk-in
   youth shelter.** Cray Youth & Family Services' own site gives
   430 Court St, New Castle, (724) 656-9029, for youth ages 10–18 removed
   from home due to abuse, neglect or out-of-control behaviours. The
   "Temporarily Closed" flag on one place listing for the old Highland Ave
   entry is NOT a closure finding.
7. **For Lawrence County veterans there are THREE SSVF providers — and the
   Veterans Leadership Program DOES serve Lawrence County** (intake
   (844) 857-8387). The other two are LCCAP itself, under its legal name
   Lawrence County Social Services, Inc. (intake (724) 658-7258 /
   1-888-252-5104), and Soldier On (intake (866) 406-8449, remote intake,
   no Lawrence County office).
8. **LCCAP takes morning walk-ins — PA 211 says "call for intake" only.**
   LCCAP's own site: walk into 241 W Grant St, New Castle, 8:30am–
   11:00am, no appointment needed; or call (724) 658-7258 for an afternoon
   appointment; Client Portal available 24/7.
9. **No Family Promise affiliate and no standalone winter/Code Blue
   overnight shelter serve Lawrence County.** Every "Family Promise of
   Lawrence" result is Lawrence, Kansas. The winter cover found is the
   City Rescue Mission's 24/7 shelter doors plus a one-off emergency
   warming centre at George Washington School during the January 2026
   power outage. These are gaps in provision, not listing errors.

## Method and limits

- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-22. No shelter, no
  directory, and no one else was contacted.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Items that could not be confirmed from an
  organisation's own site are marked CLARIFIED or UNVERIFIED — a gap in
  public web evidence, not a finding.
- Direct page-text fetch was NOT rate-limited on 2026-11-22: the City
  Rescue Mission's own Men's and Women's Ministry pages and the Cray
  Youth & Family Services Krause Youth Center page were fetched in full.
  Own-site claims for Arise, LCCAP, the Salvation Army, VLP and the
  Housing Authority rest on those organisations' own pages as returned by
  search on 2026-11-22, not on full page fetches. A live-browser re-check
  of the own sites (and of PA 211) is outstanding before any send.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed.** A failed or missing page
  is reported as unverified, never as a closure — including the Krause
  Youth Shelter entry flagged "Temporarily Closed" at its old address,
  and the City Rescue Mission's Covenant House / Covenant Cottage
  programmes, which are UNVERIFIED, not closed.
- **Bed availability is never published as a static fact.** Only intake
  phones and intake processes are reported.
- **Confidential locations:** 1 item is counted only / listed by hotline
  and business phone only — Arise (domestic-violence emergency shelter,
  confidential location). No shelter address for it appears in these files.
- **Honest limit:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text
  fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial
  pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus
  organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser
  PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.
- Every in-county address was verified by exact geocoding, not ZIP code:
  319 S Croton Ave, 1018 Rebecca St, 241 W Grant St, 430 Court St,
  2656 Ellwood Rd, 481 Neshannock Ave, 240 W Grant St,
  439 Countyline St, 708 Highland Ave and 332 Highland Ave, all New
  Castle, and 511 Lawrence Ave, Ellwood City, all geocode to Lawrence
  County. (One geocoder quirk, not a finding: geocoding "319 South Croton
  Avenue" returned the point as "319 N Croton Ave"; every source says
  South Croton Avenue.)
