## Headline corrections

- **Neighborhood Legal Services' Lawrence County office has MOVED to 101 South Mercer Street, Suite 105, New Castle, PA 16101** — not 125 East North Street, Suite 329, Temple Building, as Justia, PALawHELP and older NLS material still give. Phone unchanged: (724) 658-2677 / toll-free 1-866-761-6572.
- **NLS intake is Mon–Thu 9:00 AM–12:00 PM only — no Friday live intake, no Friday public office hours.** PALawHELP's Mon–Fri 9:00 AM–1:00 PM intake hours are stale on both the days and the close. Offices are open to the public Mon–Thu 9:00 AM–12:00 PM and 1:00–3:00 PM.
- **Laurel Legal Services no longer exists separately (merged into Summit Legal Aid, July 1, 2023), and Summit's general legal aid does NOT serve Lawrence County.** The correct regional civil legal-aid provider for Lawrence is **Neighborhood Legal Services** (Allegheny, Beaver, Butler and Lawrence Counties).
- **Summit's Fair Housing Law Center DOES serve Lawrence County** — a separate, housing-discrimination-only project (10 W. Cherry Ave., Washington, PA 15301; (724) 225-6170; toll-free 1-877-725-4472) covering 32 counties including Lawrence. Do not collapse it into Summit general intake.
- **Pennsylvania SeniorLAW Helpline hours are Mon–Thu 10:00 AM–12:00 PM** (SeniorLAW Center's own current pages, re-confirmed 2026-11-22), not 10 AM–2 PM as in older secondary sources. Number 1-877-727-7529, for Pennsylvanians 60+, is confirmed.
- **PA 211 gives the Lawrence County Area Agency on Aging a wrong ZIP in some records: 16015 instead of 16105** for 2706 Mercer Road, Shenley Square, New Castle (geocoding confirms Lawrence County, 16105-1422). Phone (724) 658-3729 is confirmed.
- **The Pennsylvania Bar Association Lawyer Referral Service is a PAID referral, not free legal aid** (initial 30-minute consultation no more than $30; current number 800-932-0311, option 2, not the superseded 800-692-7375). Lawrence County already has its own lawyer-referral office — the county PFA Coordinator and Lawyer Referral, (724) 656-1921, 430 Court Street — so the PBA number is a fallback, not the local route.
- **The Crisis Shelter of Lawrence County is now Arise.** 24-hour hotline (724) 652-9036 and free Lawrence-County-wide victim services are confirmed; the street address is withheld from this public audit (shelter facility) and must not be included in any send.

## Method and limits

Public web only — search and page-text fetch on 2026-11-22; nobody was contacted. Each listing was checked against the organisation's own website / own page where retrievable. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. No legal advice was rewritten: only contact information, structure and metadata were audited.

Limits, stated plainly:

- This is a **partial pilot, not a complete county directory** — PA 211's JavaScript search pages could not be pulled in full by text fetch. A live-browser PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.
- **No organisation was called closed or defunct.** UNVERIFIED means only that no own-site confirmation could be retrieved on 2026-11-22. Laurel Legal Services is out of scope because its successor's own site documents the 2023 merger, not because a page failed to load.
