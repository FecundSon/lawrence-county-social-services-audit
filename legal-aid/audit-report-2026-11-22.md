## Headline findings (only own-site-evidenced, material corrections)

1. **Neighborhood Legal Services' Lawrence County office has MOVED — it is now at 101 South Mercer Street, Suite 105, New Castle, PA 16101, not 125 East North Street, Suite 329, Temple Building.** NLS's own Contact page (nlsa.us/contact, retrieved 2026-11-22) gives the Lawrence office as 101 South Mercer Street, Suite 105, New Castle, PA 16101, phone (724) 658-2677, toll-free (866) 761-6572. Justia, PALawHELP (last review Feb 07, 2023) and older NLS material still give the Temple Building address. The phone number is unchanged.
2. **NLS intake hours are NOT Mon–Fri 9:00 AM–1:00 PM, and there is no Friday intake or Friday public office time.** NLS's own Contact page states intake answers calls Monday–Thursday 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. (voicemail until 1:00 p.m.; callbacks 1:00–4:30 p.m. Mon–Thu and Fridays until 4:30 p.m.), and offices are open to the public Monday–Thursday 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. and 1:00–3:00 p.m., closed noon–1:00 p.m. for lunch. PALawHELP's Mon–Fri 9:00 AM–1:00 PM intake hours are therefore stale on both the days and the close. A Lawrence resident who calls on a Friday morning expecting live intake will not get it.
3. **Laurel Legal Services no longer exists as a separate programme — it merged into Summit Legal Aid on July 1, 2023 — and Summit's general legal aid does NOT serve Lawrence County.** Summit's own site states it provides free legal advice and representation in Armstrong, Cambria, Clarion, Fayette, Greene, Indiana, Jefferson, Somerset, Washington and Westmoreland Counties only. The correct regional civil legal-aid provider for Lawrence County is **Neighborhood Legal Services** (Allegheny, Beaver, Butler and Lawrence Counties). Any directory still listing Laurel Legal Services, or Summit Legal Aid general services, for Lawrence County is stale.
4. **Summit Legal Aid's Fair Housing Law Center DOES serve Lawrence County — the exception that proves finding 3.** The Fair Housing Law Center's own site (fhlaw.org) states it operates in 32 Pennsylvania counties including Lawrence, and the retrievable PA 211 record lists Lawrence in its service area. It is a separate, housing-discrimination-only project of Summit (10 West Cherry Avenue, Washington, PA 15301; (724) 225-6170; toll-free 1-877-725-4472), not general Summit intake. Do not collapse the two: Summit general = out of scope for Lawrence; Fair Housing Law Center = in scope, housing discrimination only.
5. **Pennsylvania SeniorLAW Helpline hours are Monday–Thursday 10:00 AM–12:00 PM on SeniorLAW Center's own current pages — not 10:00 AM–2:00 PM as stated in several older secondary sources.** Re-confirmed 2026-11-22 on seniorlawcenter.org (HelpLine, Access Services and Victim Services pages). The helpline number 1-877-727-7529 and the 60+ age scope are confirmed. (Same correction as in the Snyder County audit, 2026-11-15; the older 10–2 window persists in secondary directories.)
6. **PA 211 gives the Lawrence County Area Agency on Aging a wrong ZIP in some records: 16015 instead of 16105.** PA 211 Area Agency on Aging / Long Term Care Options Counseling records list "2706 Mercer Road, Shenley Square, New Castle, PA 16015". Exact geocoding of 2706 Mercer Road, New Castle, returns Lawrence County, PA 16105-1422. The organisation's own context (Catholic Charities, SeniorLAW 2016 directory, PA Aging notices) consistently gives 16105. 16015 is a Butler-area ZIP; mail addressed to it for a New Castle office is misdirected.
7. **The Pennsylvania Bar Association Lawyer Referral Service is a PAID referral, not free legal aid — and Lawrence County already has its own lawyer-referral office, so the PBA number is a fallback, not the local route.** PBA's own Find a Lawyer page states the initial 30-minute consultation costs no more than $30, and the current number is 800-932-0311 (select option 2). Lawrence County's own PFA Coordinator and Lawyer Referral office (724-656-1921, 430 Court Street) is the local referral service. Older sources still give the superseded PBA number 800-692-7375.
8. **The Crisis Shelter of Lawrence County is now Arise — contact it by hotline, not by street address.** Arise's own site (ariselc.org) presents the organisation as "Arise" (formerly the Crisis Shelter), serving Lawrence County victims of domestic violence, sexual assault and other violent crimes since 1981, with a 24-hour hotline (724) 652-9036 and civil legal representation. Justia and WomensLaw.org (updated July 15, 2026) still list the old name. The hotline and services are confirmed. The street address is withheld from this public audit: it is the emergency shelter facility, and this audit does not repeat shelter-facility street addresses in public files, even though Arise's own site lists the address publicly and says the facility 'is clearly identified in the community'.

## What this report does NOT claim

- **Not a complete county directory.** PA 211's JavaScript search pages could not be pulled in full by text fetch; this is a partial pilot (31 rows). Other Lawrence legal-aid listings may exist on PA 211 that were not retrievable here.
- **No organisation was called closed or defunct.** UNVERIFIED means only that no own-site confirmation could be retrieved on 2026-11-22. Laurel Legal Services is out of scope because its own successor's site documents the 2023 merger, not because a page failed to load.
- **No legal advice.** Only contact information, structure and metadata were audited: names, addresses, phones, service areas, intake routes and hours, and whether a service is free, paid, or referral-only.
- **No eligibility determination.** Income thresholds quoted (e.g. PA Free Legal Answers 250% of federal poverty level) are as stated on the cited pages and change annually; they are not a finding that any particular person qualifies.
- **No in-county location is claimed on the basis of ZIP or town name alone.** Every address was checked by exact geocoding against its county: 101 S. Mercer St., 125 E. North St., 430 Court St. and 2706 Mercer Rd., New Castle, all geocode to Lawrence County (the Arise street address was not geocoded here — it is withheld from this public audit); 111 S. Cliff St., Butler, geocodes to Butler County; 10 W. Cherry Ave., Washington, geocodes to Washington County. Out-of-county offices are in scope only where the organisation's own site states it serves Lawrence County.
- **The Arise street address is withheld from this public audit.** It is the emergency shelter facility; this audit does not repeat shelter-facility street addresses in public files, even though Arise's own site lists the address publicly. Contact is by hotline (724) 652-9036 or business line (724) 652-9206.
- **Ellwood City straddles Lawrence and Beaver Counties** — no Ellwood City address in this pilot was treated as a Lawrence County location on the basis of town name alone.
- **The auditor is not a local resident of Lawrence County** and did not visit any office.
- **Nothing has been sent.** No correction has been submitted to PA 211 or to any organisation.
