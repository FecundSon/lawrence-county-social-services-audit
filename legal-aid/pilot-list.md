## How this list was built

PA 211's JavaScript search pages could not be pulled in full by text fetch on 2026-11-22. The pilot below was therefore assembled from:

1. Retrievable PA 211 search-result pages (Fair Housing Law Center, Summit Legal Aid, MidPenn Legal Services, Lawrence County Area Agency on Aging / PA Link records) — search.pa211.org
2. Justia — Lawrence County Legal Aid & Pro Bono Services — justia.com/lawyers/pennsylvania/lawrence-county/legal-aid-and-pro-bono-services
3. Neighborhood Legal Services own site (nlsa.us) and PALawHELP.org organisation directory — palawhelp.org
4. Lawrence County government own site (lawrencecountypa.gov) — Courts, PFA–Lawyer Referral, Domestic Relations, Public Defender, Sheriff, departments pages
5. SeniorLAW Center — Pennsylvania Legal Resource Directory for all Pennsylvania Counties (October 2016 edition, Lawrence County section) — seniorlawcenter.org
6. PA Department of Health / PA.gov — Lawrence County Resource Guide (updated December 2019) — pa.gov
7. WomensLaw.org local programmes (updated July 15, 2026) and Arise (ariselc.org) — for the former Crisis Shelter of Lawrence County
8. Pennsylvania Legal Aid Network / Carnegie Library legal-organisations listing — for the regional legal-aid map (NLS / Summit / MidPenn / Northwestern)

No listing below was assumed correct. Each entry records the claim **as found**; verification against the organisation's own site is in `audit-report-2026-11-22.md`. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator.

## Geocoding / scope notes (as found)

- **New Castle, Ellwood City (Lawrence County part), New Wilmington, Wampum, Bessemer, Volant, Pulaski and Enon Valley are in Lawrence County.** All street addresses in New Castle were verified by exact geocoding against Lawrence County (see audit report): 101 S. Mercer St., 125 E. North St., 430 Court St. and 2706 Mercer Rd. all geocode to Lawrence County. The Arise street address is withheld from this public audit (shelter facility) and was not geocoded here.
- **Butler (Butler County), Pittsburgh (Allegheny County), Washington (Washington County), Harrisburg (Dauphin County), Greensburg (Westmoreland County), Clarion (Clarion County), Erie (Erie County) and Philadelphia (Philadelphia County) are OUT of Lawrence County.** Listings based there are in scope only where the organisation's own site states it serves Lawrence County residents (e.g. NLS Pittsburgh HQ serving Lawrence; Fair Housing Law Center; statewide helplines). An address in another county is not, by itself, a reason to exclude a listing — service area is the test.
- **Ellwood City straddles Lawrence and Beaver Counties** — an Ellwood City address does not by itself prove a Lawrence County location; the only Ellwood City item in this pilot is a private firm cited as an example in row 30, not a legal-aid listing.
- **Laurel Legal Services, Summit Legal Aid, MidPenn Legal Services and Northwestern Legal Services** were included because they appear in retrievable PA 211 results or statewide legal-aid lists that a Lawrence County searcher could reasonably reach; their service areas were to be verified, not assumed. Neighbouring Mercer County is served by Northwestern Legal Services, not by NLS — the county line matters here.
- **VOICE (Butler) and Crisis Center North (Pittsburgh)** were included because Justia lists them for Lawrence County; their service areas were to be verified, not assumed.
