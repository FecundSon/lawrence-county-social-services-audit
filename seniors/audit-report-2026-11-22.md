## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
1. **PA 211's ZIP code for the Lawrence County AAA is wrong in
   every retrievable record.** PA 211 lists the Lawrence County
   Area Agency on Aging (and its protective-services, ombudsman,
   in-home-services and PA LINK records) at "2706 Mercer Road,
   Shenley Square, New Castle, PA **16015**". The correct ZIP is
   **16105**: the agency's own site (ccpgh.org/services/
   senior-services), the PA Department of Aging 2026 Senior FMNP
   agency list, and exact geocoding of 2706 Mercer Rd all give
   New Castle, PA 16105, Lawrence County. 16015 is not a New
   Castle ZIP. Correct every Lawrence County AAA record to 16105.
2. **The PA Code still lists the AAA's old address.** 6 Pa. Code
   § 30.24, Appendix A, lists the Lawrence County Area Agency on
   Aging at **20 South Mercer Street, New Castle, PA 16101**. The
   current office is **2706 Mercer Rd, Shenley Square, New Castle,
   PA 16105**, (724) 658-3729, Mon–Fri 7:30am–4:30pm (own site).
   Any listing still showing 20 South Mercer Street is stale.
3. **Meals on Wheels of New Castle — two phones and a stale old
   address in circulation.** The organisation's own site
   (mealsonwheelsofnewcastle.org) gives physical address **3345
   Wilmington Rd, New Castle, PA 16105** (mailing: PO Box 5122,
   New Castle, PA 16105) and the Program Coordinator number
   **724-674-2320** (mownc1@gmail.com). Its Form 990 and
   aggregators give main phone **(724) 652-2365**. Aggregators
   still list the old Salvation Army address **240 W Grant St,
   New Castle, with (724) 654-6155** — the programme's own site
   says it originally operated out of the Salvation Army building
   on Grant Street and now works in partnership with UPMC
   Nutritional Services. Publish 3345 Wilmington Rd and resolve
   which of the two current numbers is the client line; drop the
   Grant Street address/phone.
4. **PA 211 protective-services record has a typo as well as the
   wrong ZIP.** Its eligibility text reads "Serves **Lawrecence**
   County residents" and the address carries ZIP 16015. The local
   Older Adult Protective Services number **(724) 658-3729** is
   confirmed by PA 211, the PA Department of Aging's elder-abuse
   reporting lists, and UPMC Horizon policy documents. Correct
   the spelling to Lawrence and the ZIP to 16105.
5. **PA 211's ombudsman record links a superseded URL.** The
   Lawrence County Long-Term Care Ombudsman Services record gives
   the website as **ccpgh.org/pages/services/senior-services**.
   The current page is **ccpgh.org/services/senior-services**.
   The programme itself is confirmed on the agency's own site;
   no separate ombudsman intake number was found — the general
   AAA number (724) 658-3729 is the published route.
6. **Only two senior centres serve Lawrence County — New Castle
   and Ellwood City.** The agency's own site lists Challenges
   centres at Shenley Square, 2706 Mercer Rd, New Castle, and
   1405 Woodside Ave, Ellwood City (geocoded: Lawrence County),
   only. No centre in New Wilmington, Volant, Wampum or Bessemer
   was found. A secondary AI-generated county guide claiming a
   congregate meal site in Volant is not supported by any agency
   source found and that page contains other errors (see below) —
   do not publish a Volant site.
7. **Farmers Market vouchers — 2026 season ends Nov 30, 2026.**
   PA Department of Aging 2026 agency list: Lawrence County is
   **in-person distribution only** through Challenges, Options in
   Aging, 2706 Mercer Rd, New Castle, PA 16105, (724) 658-3729.
   On the check date (Nov 22, 2026) the season is eight days from
   ending; do not publish this as an open programme without a
   fresh 2027 check.
8. **PA LINK hours conflict between PA 211 records.** The AAA
   record gives Mon–Fri **7:30am–4:30pm**; the PA LINK record for
   the same address and phone gives Mon–Fri **8:30am–4:30pm**.
   The agency's own site gives 7:30am–4:30pm for the New Castle
   centre. Publish 7:30am and correct the PA LINK record; the
   PA LINK record's anrinfo.org website link also needs a current
   check.
9. **LIFE Lawrence County — own-site hours conflict.** VieCare's
   community page gives **8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.**; its locations archive
   gives **8 a.m.–5 p.m.** Address and phone agree everywhere:
   **2911 W. State St, New Castle, PA 16101, (724) 657-8800**,
   TTY 711 (own site; PA Dept of Aging LIFE providers list,
   revised 08/2026). Flag the closing time for a further check.
