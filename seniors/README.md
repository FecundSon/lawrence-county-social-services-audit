## Method and limits
Pilot list built from retrievable PA 211 records, Catholic Charities'
Challenges: Options in Aging own site (ccpgh.org), ACTS own site
(rideactsbus.org), NCATA own site (newcastletransit.org), Meals on
Wheels of New Castle own site (mealsonwheelsofnewcastle.org),
VieCare own site (lifeviecare.org), PA Department of Aging
publications, and secondary directories. Every item was then checked
against the organisation's own website/page via public web search
and page-text fetch. Own site beats directory; directory beats a
third-party aggregator. No organisation was contacted. A failed or
missing page is UNVERIFIED, never closure — no organisation is
called closed or defunct. Addresses were verified by exact
geocoding, not ZIP or town name alone; Ellwood City straddles
Lawrence and Beaver counties (1405 Woodside Ave geocodes to
Lawrence); Sharon (Buhl Blvd) is Mercer County and out of scope;
Lawrence County, Kentucky and Ohio results were excluded. Contact
information and listing accuracy only — no health, benefits or
legal advice.
