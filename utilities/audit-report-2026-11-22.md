## Headline findings (only own-site-evidenced, material corrections)

1. **Lawrence County Assistance Office MOVED — it is no longer at 108 Cascade Galleria.** The state's own County Assistance Offices page (checked 2026-11-22) lists **Lawrence County Assistance Office, 2656 Ellwood Road, Suite 103, New Castle, PA 16101, phone 724-656-3000, LIHEAP 724-656-3021, toll-free 1-800-847-4522, Mon–Fri 7:30 AM–5:00 PM** (geocoded to New Castle, Lawrence County, 40.96321, -80.30741). Lawrence County Commissioners' meeting minutes of September 22, 2026 record that the office would close its 108 Cascade Galleria location after September 25 and open at 2656 Ellwood Road, Suite 103, on September 28. Every directory retrieved in this pilot — PA 211, welfareoffices.org, tanfprogram.com, snapoffices.com — still lists 108 Cascade Galleria. Phones are unchanged.
2. **LIHEAP is OPEN for 2026–27 — any listing still showing "LIHEAP closed" is stale.** PA DHS's own page, checked 2026-11-22: "The 2026-2027 LIHEAP season is open! The season runs November 2, 2026 through April 23, 2027." Cash grants $200–$1,000; crisis grants $25 minimum, $1,000 season maximum; income ≤150% FPIG; apply via COMPASS; hotline 1-866-857-7095 Mon–Fri. A third-party checker (CornerHelp) showed the Lawrence County Assistance Office LIHEAP entry as "Closed for the season", confirmed October 22 and October 26, 2026 — accurate before the November 2 opening, stale now. Pennsylvania's annual winter shutoff moratorium runs December 1 through March 31, preventing regulated electric and gas utilities from terminating service to households at or below 250% of the federal poverty level (PA DHS announcement, November 2, 2026).
3. **PPL does not serve Lawrence County — the "LCCAP administers PPL WRAP" claim is the wrong utility entirely.** Penn Power (FirstEnergy) is the electric utility for Lawrence County (FirstEnergy: Penn Power serves all or parts of Allegheny, Beaver, Butler, Crawford, Lawrence and Mercer counties). The aggregator needhelppayingbills.com claims LCCAP "administers the Pennsylvania Power and Light Winter Relief Assistance Program (WRAP)". PPL Electric serves no part of Lawrence County. The Penn Power equivalent is the FirstEnergy WARM programme (see U-07). Do not list PPL OnTrack, WRAP or Operation HELP as Lawrence County programmes.
4. **FirstEnergy WARM intake is 1-888-282-6816 (Dollar Energy Fund), not 1-888-406-8074.** FirstEnergy's own current WARM page (last modified June 4, 2026): apply online or "Call the Dollar Energy Fund at 1-888-282-6816"; income at or below 200% of 2026 Federal Poverty Guidelines ($31,920 for a 1-person household, $66,000 for 4); average usage at least 500 kWh/month, minimum 6,000 kWh/year. 1-888-406-8074 is FirstEnergy's older Human Services Department number, still circulating in PUC booklets and secondary guides. The older NLSA figure 1-800-207-9276 for Penelec/Penn Power WARM is likewise superseded on FirstEnergy's own page.
5. **Salvation Army New Castle is in ZIP 16101, not 16103, with limited weekday hours.** The Salvation Army's own location page: **240 West Grant Street, New Castle, PA 16101, phone 724-652-7921, Mon–Thu 9:00 AM–2:00 PM, Fri 9:00 AM–12:00 PM**. Several directories give the ZIP as 16103. It is the Salvation Army unit for New Castle / Lawrence County and a Project REACH distribution point (see U-08).
6. **"Columbia Gas HeatShare" as described in aggregators is an Ohio programme, not a Pennsylvania one — do not list it for Lawrence County.** The HeatShare details circulating for Lawrence County (income up to 300% FPIG, apply via SAHelp.com, "those who qualify for PIPP or Emergency HEAP must first access HEAP") are from the Salvation Army's Northeast Ohio Division pages for Columbia Gas of Ohio; PIPP and HEAP are Ohio programmes. No Columbia Gas of Pennsylvania HeatShare programme was found on Columbia Gas PA's own site or on Salvation Army Western Pennsylvania pages. The Pennsylvania Columbia Gas hardship route is the Dollar Energy Fund (see U-06).
7. **PA American Water H2O Help to Others — PA 211's figures are stale.** PA 211's H2O record gives income at 150% FPIG ($1,361/month for 1 person) and a 65% monthly service-charge discount. Pennsylvania American Water's own customer-assistance page (checked 2026-11-22) gives grants of up to $500 per household per year, 22%–82% off monthly water charges and 37%–85% off monthly wastewater charges, $40/month forgiven from past-due balances, income up to 250% FPIG ($3,325/month for 1 person), applied for via the Dollar Energy Fund at 1-888-282-6816. PA 211's service area (including Lawrence, PA) is correct; its income and discount figures are not.
8. **LIHWAP is closed — do not list it as available.** LIHWAP was a temporary federal water-assistance programme; federal funds were exhausted and the programme closed out in 2023–2024 (ACF Office of Community Services: funding no longer available, no new benefits). There is no current LIHWAP application round. (Water-bill help in PA now runs via utility hardship programmes, e.g. Dollar Energy Fund / H2O Help to Others — see U-06 and U-20.)
9. **"Peoples Gas CAP" is the wrong gas utility for Lawrence County.** A PA 211 record for Catholic Charities says it screens for the "Peoples Gas CAP Program". Peoples Natural Gas serves the Pittsburgh area and, via an exchange with Columbia Gas at New Castle, the Grove City area of Mercer County — not Lawrence County. Lawrence County's gas utility is Columbia Gas of Pennsylvania, whose CAP is at 1-800-537-7431 (see U-09).
