## Notes on the pilot as found

- The Lawrence County electric utility is Penn Power (FirstEnergy), not PPL Electric; the gas utility in New Castle / Lawrence County is Columbia Gas of Pennsylvania. Statewide lists that name PPL or UGI programmes for Lawrence County need a territory check before they are treated as Lawrence listings (see U-23, U-24).
- The Lawrence County Assistance Office address in directories is consistently 108 Cascade Galleria, New Castle; that address is checked in the audit report against the state's own CAO page (U-03).
- The Salvation Army New Castle ZIP circulates as both 16101 and 16103; checked in the audit report (U-16).
- No Lawrence-specific LIHWAP programme was found in this pilot; LIHWAP is included only to test whether it still appears as an available programme (U-22).
- Ellwood City locations (the city straddles Lawrence and Beaver counties) were not separately audited in this pilot.
