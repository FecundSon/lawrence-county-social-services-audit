## Headline corrections

1. **Lawrence County Assistance Office MOVED to 2656 Ellwood Road, Suite 103, New Castle, PA 16101** — it closed 108 Cascade Galleria after September 25, 2026 and opened at Ellwood Road on September 28 (county commissioners' minutes, Sept 22, 2026; pa.gov CAO page). Phones unchanged: 724-656-3000, LIHEAP 724-656-3021. Every directory retrieved still lists Cascade Galleria.
2. **LIHEAP is OPEN for 2026–27 (Nov 2, 2026–Apr 23, 2027; cash $200–$1,000; crisis $25–$1,000 season max)** — any listing still showing "LIHEAP closed" (e.g. a third-party checker, confirmed Oct 22/26, 2026) is stale. Winter shutoff moratorium Dec 1–Mar 31 for households ≤250% FPL.
3. **PPL does not serve Lawrence County** — the aggregator claim that LCCAP "administers the PPL WRAP" is the wrong utility entirely. The electric utility is Penn Power (FirstEnergy); the equivalent programme is WARM (U-07). Do not list PPL OnTrack / WRAP / Operation HELP for Lawrence County.
4. **FirstEnergy WARM intake is 1-888-282-6816 (Dollar Energy Fund), not 1-888-406-8074** (older FirstEnergy number still in PUC booklets) or 1-800-207-9276. Income ≤200% of 2026 FPIG ($31,920 for 1), usage ≥500 kWh/month.
5. **Salvation Army New Castle is in ZIP 16101, not 16103** — 240 West Grant Street, New Castle, PA 16101, phone 724-652-7921, Mon–Thu 9 AM–2 PM, Fri 9 AM–12 PM (Salvation Army's own page).
6. **"Columbia Gas HeatShare" as described in aggregators is an Ohio programme, not a Pennsylvania one** (300% FPIG, SAHelp.com, PIPP/HEAP are Ohio) — do not list it for Lawrence County. The Pennsylvania Columbia Gas hardship route is the Dollar Energy Fund.
7. **PA American Water H2O Help to Others: PA 211's figures are stale** — current own-site figures are income up to 250% FPIG ($3,325/month for 1), grants up to $500/year, discounts 22%–82% water / 37%–85% wastewater, via Dollar Energy Fund 1-888-282-6816 — not 150% / $1,361 / 65% as PA 211 lists.
8. **LIHWAP is closed** (federal programme ended 2023–24) — do not list it as open.
9. **"Peoples Gas CAP" is the wrong gas utility for Lawrence County** — a PA 211 Catholic Charities record says it screens for Peoples Gas CAP; Lawrence County's gas utility is Columbia Gas (CAP 1-800-537-7431). Peoples serves the Pittsburgh area and, via a Columbia exchange, Grove City in Mercer County.
10. **Columbia Gas CAP: list 1-800-537-7431** as the CAP enrolment line (Columbia's own CAP page and the PUC); 1-888-460-4332 is Columbia's general customer-care number, which its own fact sheet also prints for CAP.

## Method and limits

Public web only (search + page-text fetch) on 2026-11-22. No organisation was contacted. Own site beats directory; directory beats third-party aggregator. A failed or missing page is UNVERIFIED, never closure; no programme is called defunct. Seasonal/funding statuses are date-stamped to 2026-11-22. Addresses were verified by exact geocoding: 2656 Ellwood Rd, 108 Cascade Galleria, 1745 Frew Mill Rd, 241 W Grant St, 240 W Grant St, 6 N Mill St, 920 N Cedar St and 119 E North St are all in New Castle, Lawrence County; 2706 Mercer Rd is in New Castle, Lawrence County, ZIP 16105 (not 16015 as PA 211 gives for the Catholic Charities senior-services record). All in-county offices checked are in New Castle. Ellwood City locations (the city straddles Lawrence and Beaver counties) were not separately audited. The Lawrence County electric utility is Penn Power (FirstEnergy) and the gas utility is Columbia Gas of Pennsylvania; PPL, UGI and Peoples Natural Gas do not serve Lawrence County.
