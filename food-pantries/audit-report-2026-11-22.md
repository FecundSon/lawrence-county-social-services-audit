**Category:** Food pantries / food distributions
**Date checked:** 2026-11-22
**Method:** Public web search and page-text fetch only. Each listing was checked against the organisation's own website / own page (including its own Facebook page). Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Every address was verified by exact geocoding (map.geocode, full street address), never by ZIP or town name alone. No organisation, pantry, or directory was contacted.
**Status: NOT SENT YET.** Nothing in this report has been sent to statewide PA 211, the Greater Pittsburgh Community Food Bank, Lawrence County Community Action Partnership, or any pantry. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County.
**Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).**

## What this report does NOT claim

- It does not claim to be a complete directory of Lawrence County food pantries. PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites. A live-browser PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send, and it may surface pantries not in this pilot (in particular any PA 211 Food Pantries taxonomy entries that text fetch could not retrieve, and any pantries in Wampum, Volant, Pulaski, New Bedford, Bessemer, Hillsville, West Pittsburg, Edinburg, or Harlansburg, for which this pilot found none).
- It does not claim any pantry is closed or defunct. Wilmington (School Pantry) (#17) and the Salvation Army Ellwood City Service Center (#18) are UNVERIFIED — that is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding — and must not be published as fact.
- It does not claim hours are current beyond 2026-11-22. Pantry hours change for holidays: New Life Breadbasket's December 19, 2026 distribution is the Saturday before Christmas; the N.O.W. Project's December 2026 date was not found (its November 21, 2026 distribution was the day before this check); Holy Spirit's St. Francis pantry is relocating (see #7); LCCAP's senior boxes follow the first Thursday of the month, so the December 2026 senior-box date needs confirming.
- It does not claim the phone numbers flagged as conflicting are settled: Allegheny Wesleyan (724) 652-2173 vs 724-601-7527; Church of Genesis 724-652-0889 vs 724-654-0889; Salvation Army Ellwood City (724) 691-8012 vs (724) 752-3059; People in Need's own page prints "725-657-3303" against (724) 657-3303 everywhere else; LCCAP SNAP (724) 658-7664 vs (724) 658-7258.
- It does not claim the addresses flagged as conflicting are settled: Salvation Army New Castle 240 vs 204 W Grant St (240 is used here, on the Salvation Army's own evidence); Salvation Army Beaver Falls 721 13th St vs 414 16th St (out of scope, for the Beaver County audit); New Life Breadbasket 374 Produce St (distribution) vs 346 E Washington St (church).
- It does not rewrite expert advisory content. Only contact information, structure, and metadata (names, addresses, phones, hours, eligibility, meal-vs-pantry and monthly-vs-weekly distinctions, duplicates, county) were audited.
- A live website does not by itself prove a pantry is currently operating. Hours from a church/office/thrift page are not pantry hours, and are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible (notably People in Need office hours, Church of Genesis thrift-store hours, Holy Spirit parish office hours, and the Divine Grace 4:00pm finder hours).
