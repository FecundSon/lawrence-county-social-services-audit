# Lawrence County, PA — Food Pantry Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-22. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, the Greater Pittsburgh Community
Food Bank, Lawrence County Community Action Partnership, or any pantry.
No organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 21 in-scope food pantry / food distribution
listings for Lawrence County, PA (county seat New Castle; largest city
New Castle; other towns Ellwood City, New Wilmington, New Galilee,
Enon Valley, Wampum, Volant, Pulaski, New Bedford, Bessemer,
Hillsville, West Pittsburg, Edinburg, Harlansburg), plus 5
out-of-county exclusions, checked against the pantries' own websites
and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement
directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open pantries,
and it should not be used as one without checking with the pantry or
PA 211 first. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-22. No pantry,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- A pantry's own website (including its own Facebook page) is treated
  as authoritative over third-party directories. Where a pantry's own
  site did not state an address or hours, that item is marked
  UNVERIFIED in the full report — it is a gap in public web evidence,
  not a finding, and must not be published as fact.
- **No pantry is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or
  missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a pantry is currently
  operating.
- Hours from a church/office/thrift page are not pantry hours, and
  are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible (notably
  People in Need office hours, Church of Genesis thrift-store hours,
  Holy Spirit parish office hours, and the Divine Grace 4:00pm
  finder hours).
- Monthly distributions are not weekly pantries, and meals are not
  grocery pantries; both distinctions are flagged wherever they
  applied (People in Need, LCCAP senior boxes, New Beaver, Little
  Beaver, New Life Breadbasket, N.O.W. Project, City Rescue Mission
  Community Meal, Divine Grace Community Lunch, Westminster Student
  Food Pantry, Graham's Little Pantry, Mom's Meals).
- Every address was verified by exact geocoding (full street
  address), never by ZIP or town name alone. ZIP code does not
  settle county in this area: Ellwood City straddles the
  Lawrence/Beaver line — 311 Lawrence Ave is Lawrence County, but
  408 Hickernell Rd is Beaver County — and several pantries that
  appear on "near Lawrence County" lists are in fact in Beaver,
  Butler, or Mercer County, or in Ohio. They are listed as OUT OF
  SCOPE exclusions, not as Lawrence County pantries.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-22 returned only retrievable
  individual PA 211 items — no complete extractable Lawrence County
  result list. The pilot is therefore anchored on those retrievable
  PA 211 items (City Rescue Mission Family Care pantry and
  Community Meal, LCCAP Food Pantry, People in Need Food Pantry,
  Allegheny Wesleyan Food Pantry, LCCAP SNAP Assistance, and the
  Beaver Falls food bank record), LCCAP's own Food Security page
  and "Public Food Pantries" flyer, the Lawrence County Housing
  Authority's "Additional Food Resources" document, HelpByState's
  Lawrence County page (last checked October 9, 2026), the Greater
  Pittsburgh Community Food Bank locator as republished by
  FoodBank.us (updated 2026-09-11), and the organisations' own
  sites, each item labelled with its claim source. A live-browser
  check of search.pa211.org for Lawrence County is still outstanding
  before any send to PA 211.
- Holiday hours matter and are date-stamped in the full report: New
  Life Breadbasket's December 19, 2026 distribution is the Saturday
  before Christmas; the N.O.W. Project's December 2026 date was not
  found (its November 21, 2026 distribution was the day before this
  check); Holy Spirit's St. Francis pantry is relocating.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the pantries themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
