# Lawrence County, PA — Food Pantries Pilot List

**County:** Lawrence County, PA (county seat New Castle; largest city New Castle; other towns Ellwood City, New Wilmington, New Galilee, Enon Valley, Wampum, Volant, Pulaski, New Bedford, Bessemer, Hillsville, West Pittsburg, Edinburg, Harlansburg)
**Category:** Food pantries / food distributions
**Date checked:** 2026-11-22
**Method:** Public web search and page-text fetch only. Each listing was checked against the organisation's own website / own page (including its own Facebook page). Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Every address was verified by exact geocoding (map.geocode, full street address), never by ZIP or town name alone. No organisation, pantry, or directory was contacted.
**Status: NOT SENT YET.** Nothing in this pilot list has been sent to statewide PA 211, the Greater Pittsburgh Community Food Bank, Lawrence County Community Action Partnership, or any pantry. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County.
**Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).**

## Honest limit

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.

## What this pilot list does NOT claim

- It does not claim to be a complete directory of Lawrence County food pantries. In particular, no pantry was found in this pilot for Wampum, Volant, Pulaski, New Bedford, Bessemer, Hillsville, West Pittsburg, Edinburg, or Harlansburg — that is a gap in this pilot, not a finding that none exists. A live-browser PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding and may surface pantries not in this pilot.
- It does not claim any pantry is closed or defunct. Items marked UNVERIFIED in the audit report are gaps in public web evidence, not findings, and must not be published as fact.
- Hours are as found on 2026-11-22. Monthly distributions shift around holidays: the N.O.W. Project's December 2026 date was not found, and New Life Breadbasket's December 19, 2026 date is the Saturday before Christmas.
