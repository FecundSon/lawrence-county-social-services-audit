## Headline corrections

1. **MATP administrator is Lawrence County Social Services, Inc. (LCSS), under the LCCAP umbrella — booking (724) 658-7258.** LCCAP's own page says LCCAP administers MATP, and the county's 2026–27 agreement names LCSS as the vendor/administrator. Same partnership, not two administrators: PA 211 should file one MATP entry (241 W Grant St, New Castle, PA 16101) under LCSS/LCCAP. MATP trips are delivered by ACTS (door-to-door), NCATA (bus tickets) and Noga Wheelchair Transport, plus $0.25/mile reimbursement. MATP is medical-only.
2. **Senior passes ARE valid on the Pittsburgh commuter bus — the old fares document says they are not.** NCATA's current own site: senior passes valid on all routes including the Route 71 Pittsburgh commuter; fare $4.00 per person regardless of age. The archived 2014 NCATA fares PDF says senior ID cards are NOT valid on the commuter or Route 81 — stale, contradicted by the operator's current site.
3. **PA 211's ACTS Senior Shared Ride record appears to swap voice and fax.** Operator's own site: customer service (724) 652-5588, fax (724) 546-5160. The PA 211 Senior record has them reversed (the PA 211 PwD record has them the right way round). Booking number is (724) 652-5588.
4. **ACTS PwD fare: PA 211's "$2.00 to $7.50" overstates the operator's own-site figure.** ACTS' own site: PwD passengers in Lawrence County pay an average of $2.00 to $3.00 per trip (PennDOT pays up to 85%). PA 211's $2.00–$7.50 range is not corroborated.
5. **Lawrence County Area Agency on Aging: PA 211's ZIP is wrong (16015 → 16105).** PA 211 lists 2706 Mercer Rd, New Castle, PA 16015. The organisation's own site gives 16105, and exact geocoding returns New Castle, PA 16105, Lawrence County. Phone (724) 658-3729 confirmed. The AAA (Challenges: Options in Aging) is a referral/sponsor for $1.00 senior ACTS trips, not a carrier.
6. **NCATA's local adult fare cannot be verified — do not publish $1.00 or $0.75 as current.** NCATA's own site publishes no local fare table (only seniors 65+ free and the $4.00 Pittsburgh commuter fare). A 2021 SPC report gives $1.00; a 2010 archived NCATA page says $0.75. Both are stale secondary figures.
7. **Greyhound in New Castle is a Park & Ride bus stop, not a station — and Route 91 is no Youngstown link.** Greyhound: New Castle Park & Ride, 3125 New Butler Rd, (800) 231-2222, Location Type: Bus Stop. NCATA Route 91 ends at the Ohio line; NCATA buses cannot enter Ohio and the nearest Youngstown WRTA route ends ~3 miles short — no cross-border connection.
8. **ACTS shared ride is advance-reservation, not a walk-on bus; MATP is medical-only.** ACTS reservations close 2:00 PM the business day before (Friday for Monday); full fares $13.25–$50.00 by mileage; hours Mon–Fri 5:00 AM–7:00 PM, Sat 6:00 AM–4:00 PM, closed Sundays. Same-day trips only under the PennDOT pilot for enrolled Senior/PwD passengers (max 10/month, Mon–Fri, in-county).
