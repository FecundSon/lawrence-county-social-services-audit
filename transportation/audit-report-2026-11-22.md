# Lawrence County, PA — Transportation audit

Date checked: 2026-11-22. Status: NOT SENT YET — staged only. Nothing in this report has been submitted to PA 211 or to any organisation.
County: Lawrence County, PA (county seat New Castle, largest city; other towns New Wilmington, Volant, Wampum, Bessemer, New Beaver, Enon Valley, Pulaski, Hillsville, West Pittsburg; Ellwood City is partly in Lawrence County, partly in Beaver County).
Category: Transportation.
Directory: statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
Method: for each listing, the directory / secondary claim was recorded first (pilot-list.md) and checked against the organisation's OWN website where possible. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. Public web only (browser.search + browser.open page-text fetch). No live browser tasks, no sign-ins, no purchases, no contact with any organisation. All fares, schedules and booking rules are date-stamped 2026-11-22 and are not permanent facts.
Auditor note: the auditor is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County. Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

> **Honest limit.** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch cannot pull a complete county-filtered list, so this is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Lawrence County check is outstanding before any send.

## Headline findings

- **27 listings audited: 5 CONFIRMED, 4 CORRECTED, 10 CLARIFIED, 2 UNVERIFIED, 6 OUT OF SCOPE. In scope: 21.**
- **Fixed-route operator — New Castle Area Transit Authority (NCATA).** The public fixed-route provider for Lawrence County is NCATA, 311 Mahoning Ave, New Castle, PA 16102 (geocoded: Lawrence County), (724) 654-3130, newcastletransit.org. Local routes 1–8, 11, 77, 92, 93 serve New Castle and its townships; routes 71 (Pittsburgh), 75 (New Wilmington / Volant / Grove City), 76 (Ellwood City), 81 (Boyers / Iron Mountain via Slippery Rock) and 82 (Hermitage) run out of county. Seniors 65+ ride free on all NCATA routes with a Senior Transit Pass.
- **Shared-ride operator — ACTS (Allied Coordinated Transportation Services, Inc.).** The county's shared-ride / paratransit provider is ACTS, 701 N Mercer St, New Castle, PA 16101 (geocoded: Lawrence County), customer service (724) 652-5588, toll-free 1-888-252-5104, rideactsbus.org. ACTS is a door-to-door, advance-reservation service open to the general public, serving all of Lawrence County plus weekday destinations in Butler, Beaver, Mercer and Allegheny counties. Hours Mon–Fri 5:00 AM–7:00 PM, Sat 6:00 AM–4:00 PM, closed Sundays and major holidays. Reservations by 2:00 PM one business day before (Friday by 2:00 PM for Monday trips). Full fares by mileage, $13.25 (0–1.9 miles) to $50.00 (30+ miles), 2026-11-22.
- **MATP administrator — Lawrence County Social Services, Inc. (LCSS), under the LCCAP umbrella; booking (724) 658-7258.** LCCAP's own transportation page says "LCCAP administers the Medical Assistance Transportation Program (MATP) on behalf of Lawrence County", and the county's 2026–27 MATP vendor agreement (Commissioners' minutes, June 2, 2026) names Lawrence County Social Services, Inc. as the program administrator/vendor for July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027. LCSS is one of the three agencies that make up LCCAP, so the two names describe the same partnership, not two administrators — but PA 211 should file MATP under LCSS/LCCAP with the single booking number (724) 658-7258, 241 W Grant St, New Castle, PA 16101 (geocoded: Lawrence County). MATP trips themselves are delivered by ACTS (door-to-door provider), NCATA (bus tickets) and Noga Wheelchair Transport, plus mileage reimbursement at $0.25/mile. MATP is medical-only: travel must be to a medical provider or pharmacy billing the rider's Medical Assistance plan. Do NOT use rabbittransit, STEP or any other county's MATP number for Lawrence County.
- **Correction 1 — Senior passes ARE valid on the Pittsburgh commuter bus; an old NCATA fares document says they are not.** NCATA's current own-site admin post (newcastletransit.org, retrieved 2026-11-22) states "Senior passes can be used on all NCATA routes" and "Senior citizen ID cards are valid on the commuter buses to Pittsburgh", with the commuter fare $4.00 per person regardless of age. An archived NCATA fares page (captured 2014, still circulating as a PDF) states "Senior citizen ID cards are NOT valid on the commuter buses to Pittsburgh, as well as the Route 81 to Boyer/Iron Mountain." That older statement is stale and conflicts with the operator's current site. Own site wins; the $4.00 commuter fare itself is confirmed.
- **Correction 2 — PA 211's ACTS Senior Shared Ride record appears to swap the voice and fax numbers.** ACTS' own site (FAQ, retrieved 2026-11-22): Customer Service (724) 652-5588, Fax (724) 546-5160. The PA 211 Senior Citizen Shared Ride record lists (724) 546-5160 in the voice position and (724) 652-5588 as fax — the reverse of the operator's own site (the PA 211 PwD record has them the right way round). The booking number is (724) 652-5588.
- **Correction 3 — ACTS PwD fare: PA 211's "$2.00 to $7.50 per ride" overstates what the operator's own site says.** ACTS' own site (Programs for People with Disabilities, retrieved 2026-11-22): "PennDOT will pay up [to] 85% of the full fare. For trips within Lawrence County, passengers using the PwD Program will pay an average of $2.00 to $3.00 per trip." PA 211's $2.00–$7.50 range is not corroborated by the operator's own site and should not be published as the PwD fare.
- **Correction 4 — Lawrence County Area Agency on Aging: PA 211's ZIP is wrong (16015 → 16105).** PA 211 lists Catholic Charities / Lawrence County Area Agency on Aging at "2706 Mercer Road, Shenley Square, New Castle, PA 16015". The organisation's own site (ccpgh.org) gives 2706 Mercer Rd, New Castle, PA 16105, and exact geocoding of 2706 Mercer Rd returns New Castle, PA 16105, Lawrence County. 16015 is not a New Castle ZIP. The phone (724) 658-3729 and hours Mon–Fri 7:30 AM–4:30 PM are confirmed; the AAA is a referral/sponsoring agency for senior transport (Challenges: Options in Aging, $1.00 ACTS trips), not a carrier.
- **Clarification (systemic) — NCATA's local adult fare cannot be verified from the operator's own site; do not publish $1.00 or $0.75 as current.** NCATA's own site (riders' guide, senior passes, admin posts, retrieved 2026-11-22) publishes no local fixed-route fare table; it confirms only that seniors 65+ ride free and the Pittsburgh commuter fare is $4.00. Secondary sources conflict and are old: a 2021 Southwestern Pennsylvania Commission report gives NCATA's base fare as $1.00, while an archived NCATA page (2010) says adult fares in the New Castle area were "still only 75 cents". Neither figure is published here as a current fact. The local fare must be re-checked with NCATA before any send.
- **Clarification (systemic) — MATP is medical-only; ACTS shared ride is not a walk-on bus.** MATP covers transport to Medical Assistance–covered medical services and pharmacy only — not grocery, social or other non-medical trips — and is not general public transport. Conversely, ACTS shared ride (including Senior Shared Ride and PwD) is door-to-door, advance-reservation, application-based transport, not a fixed-route bus a rider can board without registering; reservations close 2:00 PM the business day before, and same-day trips are only available under the PennDOT pilot for already-enrolled Senior/PwD passengers (max 10/month, Mon–Fri, in-county only).
- **Clarification — Greyhound in New Castle is a Park & Ride bus stop, not a station.** Greyhound lists one stop in New Castle: "New Castle Park & Ride, 3125 New Butler Rd, New Castle, PA 16101" (geocoded: Lawrence County), Location Type: Bus Stop, (800) 231-2222. There is no Greyhound station building in New Castle. Do not list it as a station.
- **Clarification — NCATA Route 91 ends at the Ohio line with no Youngstown connection.** Route 91 runs on US 422 to the Ohio/Pennsylvania line (Country Manor Social Hall). NCATA buses cannot enter Ohio, and the nearest Youngstown WRTA route turns back about 3 miles short of the line. There is no cross-border bus connection at New Castle; do not present Route 91 as a link to Youngstown transit.
- **Clarification — DAV van: two different booking numbers are in circulation.** VA.gov's DAV vans page lists Lawrence County at (724) 656-6868 (Tue–Fri VA Pittsburgh Healthcare System; Mon Altoona VA). A county veterans secondary source instead gives (724) 477-5010 (the Butler VA number) and says to call two weeks ahead, with the van leaving the Lawrence County Government Center, 430 Court St, New Castle (geocoded: Lawrence County). Neither number is published here as the sole booking number; re-check before any send.
- **Clarification — Noga Wheelchair Transport: address and phone are not settled.** Noga's own site (nogaems.com) confirms non-emergency transport and wheelchair services countywide, and the county's 2023 MATP resolution names Noga Wheelchair Transport Service as one of three MATP providers. But secondary listings disagree: countyoffice.org gives 2615 Wilmington Rd, New Castle, PA 16105 (geocoded: Lawrence County), (724) 652-8300, while a places listing gives 1060 Butler Ave, (724) 652-6677. Noga's own site does not settle the phone on the pages retrieved. Do not publish one Noga phone as verified.
- **Clarification — the "free New Castle Transit" flyer is New Castle, Indiana, not New Castle, PA.** A flyer circulating on social media advertises FREE public transportation, door-to-door, Mon–Fri 8 AM–4 PM, call (765) 529-8113. Area code 765 is Indiana. It has nothing to do with NCATA or Lawrence County and must not be filed as a Lawrence County service.
- **No organisation was called closed or defunct.** Failed or unretrieved page loads are marked UNVERIFIED, never treated as closure.

- This is NOT a complete list of Lawrence County transportation services. PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven and text fetch returned snippets, not complete per-record detail; the pilot is anchored on PA 211 items actually retrieved plus clearly-labelled secondary lists.
- No fare, schedule or route in this report is permanent. All are date-stamped 2026-11-22. NCATA publishes no local fare table on its own site, the Same Day Trip Pilot's current status is unconfirmed, and Greyhound/NCATA times change with detours and events (Transitland showed Light Up Night early stoppages on Nov 22 itself) — every fare must be re-checked before any send or publication.
- Where figures conflicted across sources, the hierarchy was applied (own site beats directory beats aggregator) and the conflict is flagged in the entry; for Noga's phone (two conflicting secondary listings) and the DAV van booking number (VA.gov vs a county secondary source) neither figure is published here as the sole verified number.
- No organisation was called closed or defunct. A failed page load or an unretrieved detail is marked UNVERIFIED, never treated as closure. In particular, Uber/Lyft availability and Infina Health are UNVERIFIED, not "closed" and not "operating".
- No organisation was contacted, and nothing was sent to PA 211 or anyone else. This audit is staged only.
- The auditor is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County, and no residency is claimed or implied.
- Secondary sources (Transitland, pittsburghtransit.org, SPC reports, county commissioners' minutes, VA.gov, Uber, Facebook/Instagram flyers, aggregators) are labelled as secondary wherever used; they do not outrank an organisation's own site.
- County scope was judged by exact address (geocoded) / stated service area, never by ZIP or town name alone. Grove City, Hermitage, Slippery Rock and Boyers are in other counties; services based there (MCCT/SVSS) are out of scope, while NCATA routes and ACTS trips that serve those places as destinations from Lawrence County are in scope and labelled as such. Ellwood City straddles Lawrence and Beaver counties; the Challenges Ellwood City centre geocodes to Lawrence County.
