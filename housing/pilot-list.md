# Lawrence County, PA — Housing / Rental Assistance Pilot List
Date checked: 2026-11-22
County: Lawrence County, PA (county seat New Castle, largest city).
Category: housing / rental assistance.
Method: pilot list built from retrievable PA 211 listings
(search.pa211.org — JavaScript-driven; text fetch cannot pull a
complete county-filtered list), the Inglis Quick Start Housing and
Services Information guide for Lawrence County, PA DHS documents
(HAP contacts, ERAP contacts), PHFA counselling-agency lists
(April 2026), and secondary directories. Every item was then
checked against the organisation's own website/page. Own site
beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator. No
organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted. The auditor
is NOT a local resident of Lawrence County. Prepared with AI
assistance (Muse). Status: NOT SENT YET.

## Honest limit
This is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus
secondary lists plus organisations' own sites — NOT a complete
county directory. A live-browser PA 211 Lawrence County check is
outstanding before any send.
