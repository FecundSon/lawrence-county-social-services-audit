## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone
trying to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing
table below.

1. **PA 211 has LCCAP's phone and fax swapped on the Rent
   Assistance listing — swap them back.** PA 211's Rent
   Assistance record for Lawrence County Community Action
   Partnership lists (724) 658-7664 as the voice number and
   (724) 658-7258 as the fax. LCCAP's own site
   (lccap.org Homeless Services page, checked 2026-11-22) gives
   (724) 658-7258 as the phone and (724) 658-7664 as the fax,
   and PA DHS's own Homeless Assistance Program contact list
   gives the same pairing (phone 658-7258, fax 658-7664) for
   LCCAP at 241 W Grant St, New Castle. The PA 211 Coordinated
   Entry record for the same office already lists them the right
   way round. Correct the Rent Assistance record to phone
   (724) 658-7258, fax (724) 658-7664.
2. **Lawrence County Assistance Office has moved — retire
   108 Cascade Galleria.** The Lawrence County Commissioners'
   meeting minutes of September 22, 2026 record that the
   Lawrence County Assistance Office "will close its 108 Cascade
   Galleria location after September 25 and open at 2656
   Ellwood Road, Suite 103, New Castle, on September 28."
   2656 Ellwood Road geocodes to Lawrence County. PA 211 and
   most secondary directories still list 108 Cascade Galleria,
   New Castle, PA 16101, phone 724-656-3000. Adopt 2656 Ellwood
   Road, Suite 103, New Castle, PA 16101, phone 724-656-3000
   (toll-free 1-800-847-4522). One conflicting secondary report
   (a social-media post, August 1, 2026) says the office would
   move to Shenango Commons Plaza, Suite 102, from October 1;
   the commissioners' minutes are the stronger source, but the
   new address must be confirmed against PA 211 / pa.gov before
   any send (see pre-send re-check list). ESA is a DHS/CAO route
   via COMPASS; the CAO is a benefits office, not a walk-in
   housing service.
3. **Catholic Charities Lawrence County office is at 6 North
   Mill Street, New Castle — retire 119 East North Street.**
   Catholic Charities of the Diocese of Pittsburgh's own
   Lawrence County page (ccpgh.org, checked 2026-11-22) gives
   6 North Mill Street, New Castle, PA 16101, phone
   724-658-5526, hours Tuesday–Friday 9am–4pm (closed noon–
   1pm). The 119 E. North Street address appears only on
   rentassistance.org. The United Way of Lawrence County
   partner list also gives 6 N Mill St, 724-658-5526. Separately,
   PA 211 records for Catholic Charities' Lawrence County senior
   services give "2706 Mercer Road, Shenley Square, New Castle,
   PA 16015" — 2706 Mercer Road geocodes to Lawrence County
   with ZIP 16105-1422, so 16015 is a wrong ZIP; that is the
   Area Agency on Aging office (724-658-3729), not the
   basic-needs/rent-help office.
4. **LCCAP ERAP listing is stale — remove ERAP from live
   help.** PA DHS's ERAP agency-contacts PDFs (2021–2022) name
   Lawrence County Community Action Partnership, PO Box 189,
   241 W Grant St, New Castle, PA 16103, 724-658-7258, as the
   Lawrence County ERAP contact. PA DHS states ERAP1 ended
   September 30, 2022 and ERAP2 ended September 30, 2025.
   LCCAP's own current Homeless Services page
   (lccap.org, checked 2026-11-22) lists no ERAP. Any live ERAP
   listing for Lawrence County is stale and must not be repeated
   as current help.
5. **PAHAF is closed — retire the "as funds are available"
   listing.** PAHAF's own site (pahaf.org) states the programme
   is no longer accepting new applications, and that it will not
   pay or approve forward mortgage assistance for any months
   past June 2025. Any directory entry still presenting PAHAF
   as live help "as funds are available, (888) 987-2423" is
   stale; do not send anyone to PAHAF as live help. HEMAP
   (item 19) is the live foreclosure-prevention route.
6. **USDA Rural Housing — ZIP corrected, and a wrong phone
   retired.** USDA's own Pennsylvania contacts page
   (rd.usda.gov, updated 2026-07-27) gives 359 East Park Drive,
   Suite 4, Harrisburg, PA 17110-2747, main telephone
   (717) 237-2299. The Inglis Lawrence County guide gives 17111
   and 717-237-2186; retire both — 17111 is used on other USDA
   pages for the same building, but the contacts page for the
   State Office itself gives 17110-2747, and 237-2186 is a
   separate USDA single-family-housing document-delivery line,
   not the State Office main number. Regional route only;
   rural-eligibility boundaries apply.
7. **LCCAP's rent help is ONE agency under several programme
   labels, not four separate sources — and the Section 8
   waitlist status is UNRESOLVED.** PA DHS names LCCAP as the
   Lawrence County HAP agency, and PA 211 lists the same office
   (241 W Grant St, 724-658-7258) separately as Rent Assistance,
   Regional ESG, CoC permanent supportive housing, and
   Coordinated Entry. Items 3, 5, 7 and 8 in this audit are the
   same agency and the same intake route under different labels;
   Coordinated Entry (item 6) is the intake hub, not a
   rent-grant programme itself. Do not double-count them as
   separate sources of rent help. Separately, the Lawrence
   County Housing Authority's Section 8 HCV waitlist status is
   UNRESOLVED: the Authority's own site (lawrencecountyha.org,
   checked 2026-11-22) states no open/closed status, offering
   only a "Check Waiting List" link; CornerHelp (confirmed
   October 26, 2026) says Section 8 is CLOSED with public
   housing accepting applications in person, while
   section8waitlist.org says all New Castle waitlists are closed
   as of November 22, 2026. Publish NEITHER version as fact
   until the Authority's own site or office confirms it.
