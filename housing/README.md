## Method and limits
Pilot list built from retrievable PA 211 listings, the Inglis Quick
Start guide for Lawrence County, PA DHS documents (HAP and ERAP
contact lists), PHFA counselling-agency lists (April 2026), and
secondary directories; every item then checked against the
organisation's own website/page via public web search and page-text
fetches. Own site beats directory; directory beats a third-party
aggregator. No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
Addresses were checked by exact address, never by ZIP or town name
alone, with exact-address geocoding where the county was in doubt
(2656 Ellwood Rd, 2418 Wilmington Rd, 630 Crescent Ave and 708
Highland Ave geocode to Lawrence County; 293 Pinney St, Rochester
geocodes to Beaver County; 601 Indiana Ave, Farrell geocodes to
Mercer County). Ellwood City straddles Lawrence and Beaver Counties,
so its LCHA properties were checked individually. No waitlist,
funding, or seasonal status is presented as permanent — every such
status is as found on 2026-11-22 only. No organisation is called
closed or defunct: the item marked UNVERIFIED is unconfirmed, not
closed. No domestic-violence safe-house address appears anywhere in
this audit.
